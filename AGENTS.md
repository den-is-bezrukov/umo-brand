# umo-brand

React + Vite + Tailwind CSS site for UMO brand materials: the brand guide at `/`, the price-card poster generator at `/price-card` (web preview + print-ready PDF export), the dealer livery generator at `/livery` (car preview + cut files), the number plate frame generator at `/plate-frame` (the strip printed under the plate, as a PDF), the name tag generator at `/name-tag` (staff badges, as a PDF) and the business card generator at `/business-card` (a dealership's staff cards, as a PDF). Deployed to `umo.autos` via GitHub Pages on every push to `main` (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`).

## Development Server

Nothing runs automatically — start it yourself:

```bash
pnpm dev
```

This serves the app at `http://localhost:8443` (port set in `vite.config.ts`, not the Vite default). Hot reload is on; changes to source files show up immediately.

`pnpm dev:follow` (`scripts/dev-follow.mjs`) does the same and also pulls the current branch every 10 seconds (fast-forward only, reinstalling dependencies when the lockfile changes), so changes pushed to it show up in the open page without touching the terminal.

## Styling

This project uses **Tailwind CSS v4** through the `@tailwindcss/vite` plugin configured in `vite.config.ts`. `src/index.css` imports Tailwind with `@import 'tailwindcss';`. Use Tailwind utility classes directly in JSX and put global CSS or Tailwind v4 theme customization in `src/index.css`. This scaffold does not need a Tailwind config file or PostCSS config.

`src/main.tsx` imports `src/index.css`, so global font wiring belongs in `src/index.css`. Keep CSS `@import` statements first, then add any `@font-face` rules and font-family defaults there.

## Brand guide (`/`)

`src/guide/Guide.tsx` is the web version of the UMO brand guide page from Figma ([UMO | Evrone, node 4810:686](https://www.figma.com/design/i6PdEDo72Vab0cAixG0nIz/UMO-%257C-Evrone?node-id=4810-686)). Text and layout are live code; every illustration is a Figma frame exported at 2x and saved as `src/assets/guide/<name>.webp` (cwebp `-q 90`), picked up automatically by `import.meta.glob`.

To refresh an illustration, re-export that frame at 2x and overwrite the file with the same name. Export the whole section Block and crop the frame out of it: frames that stretch inside a grid/flex parent come out of Figma's direct export at the wrong size (a mostly empty canvas).

Vector schemes (clear space, minimum size, co-branding, type specimens, model lettering) are SVG files in the same folder — `img()` prefers `<name>.svg` over `<name>.webp`. They are cut out of Figma SVG exports of the parent Body nodes by `scripts/split-figma-svg.py` (node ids and crop boxes are listed at the bottom of the script). Text inside them is outlined by Figma. The lettering pictures (`lettering-model8` / `lettering-model5`: MODEL lettering over its number plate) are laid out by the script's `assemble()` from separate layer exports instead, as those frames export stretched. Photos, logo misuse examples, app/social icons and model posters stay WebP.

Фотография (anchor `photography`, after Цвет; it was Ключевой образ, `key-visual`, renamed as a key visual belongs to a campaign and is temporary, while the chapter is about the brand's photography — staging of image generation is to be added there once there's material) opens with its two principles side by side over the `keyvisual` picture, then UMO 5 and UMO 8 (`photo-umo5`, `photo-umo8`): each model's photo with its ad banners, no caption. The banners are `Poster` components: a photo-only background (`<model>-<banner|square>-bg.webp`, about 3x the Figma frame) with the title, subtitle and `UmoYandexLockup` laid over it in container-width units, so text and lockup stay sharp at any size. The backgrounds were cut from the source photos using the layer geometry in Figma; the direct export of the banner photo layers is broken the same way as stretched frames.

On phones (below `md`) landscape photos — raster `Fig`s 2:1 and wider (`isWidePhoto`) — are cropped to 4:3 with `object-cover`; `focus` sets the crop's horizontal point in % where the subject isn't central (`umo5-kv`: 72, the car without the runner's sliver). An object on a flat background (`flat`, its colour: the livery, #f6f6f6) is fitted into the 4:3 frame whole instead, never cropped. SVG schemes stay whole. The Рамка номера card is 4:3 everywhere (Figma 5008:10482): the plate in its frame, 261×66, centred on the grey.

Кобрендинг (Figma 4818:1328) is two columns, a scheme over its example, on 4:3 cards: the four 444×333 Figma cards exported as SVG (see the note in `scripts/split-figma-svg.py`: they export 684 wide, viewBox shifted by 120). The schemes open large on a click (`Zoom`, a lightbox: a click anywhere or Esc closes it, the page doesn't scroll under it).

Body copy (`Text`) takes its width from its length on the 12-column Figma grid: up to 150 characters 6 columns (432px), up to 300 — 8 (600px), longer — 9 (678px). Don't set widths by hand; texts paired side by side sit in a grid with `*:max-w-none` and fill their cells.

Vertical rhythm is fluid: `--spacing-section` (96px on a 375px phone → 216px at the 1440px Figma frame) and `--spacing-chapter` (144 → 288px) in `src/index.css`, used as `gap-section` between sections and as the extra top padding of a `Chapter`; don't hard-code breakpoint gaps between blocks. Each chapter is a `Chapter` (id, title, then its `Section`s). A section's opening picture goes above its heading, with the section anchor on the picture (Позиционирование, Фотография). Chapters stay two levels deep — chapter, then its sections. Пространства holds the brand's physical places (for now its intro with the dealership photo; the Дилерский центр section is drafted and commented out in `Guide.tsx`); Носители (anchor `materials`) the templated media (Ливрея, Прайс-карта, Рамка номера, Бейдж, later leaflets and the like), as Porsche splits Spaces from Analog media. Носители is a two-column intro and a grid of the media (`Carriers`, Figma 4844:6695), with no section per medium: each card is the medium's picture right over its ↗ row to the constructor and carries its anchor (`livery`, `business-card`, `price-card`, `name-tag`, `plate-frame`, the sidebar's sub-items); pictures 2:1 and wider span the width, the rest pair up, a narrow one moving up next to an unpaired one. The media are one list, `MEDIA_LIST` in `Guide.tsx`; `packMedia` turns it into the order the cards actually stand in (`MEDIA`), and both the grid and the sidebar's sub-items follow it. A new medium is one entry there. Anchor ids sit on an empty marker above the title, so keep new chapters inside `Chapter` rather than a bare `<h2 id>`. Links to the guide have gone out, so when an anchor id changes, add the old one to `OLD_ANCHORS` in `Guide.tsx`: it is redirected to the new id on load and on a hash change.

The first screen follows Figma 4893:3846 and 4897:4565 (prototype 4921:2352): on `lg`+ the hero (`Hero` in `Guide.tsx`) is fixed full-width behind the page, which starts a strip above the bottom of the screen (`--strip`, set by `useHeroStrip`: 286px at 1440), so the first screen shows the photo, the 200px logo, the «Содержание» row, the statement and the quick links under it (Стандарты → `#positioning`, the way into the whole guide from its first page, which lands its picture 24px under the top of the screen from `lg` — `lg:scroll-mt-6`; Носители → `#materials`, 48px below the statement; no link to the official site); scrolling, the page slides up over the photo. ## Obscene words

The plate frame, name tag, business card and livery generators refuse obscene words (`src/ui/profanity.ts`, `hasProfanity`), so a screenshot of «Центр UMO | <мат>» from umo.autos can't happen: the check adds an issue in `buildStrip`, `buildTag` (per field: «Имя: недопустимое слово») and `setText` (on the lines holding the word, so only they turn red; all of a text's lines when the word is spaced out across them), which turns the text red and blocks the download like any other error. The word itself is never shown, and nothing is starred out. It catches the Russian мат roots (хуй, пизд, еб at the start of a word or after a prefix, блядь/бля, муд-, пидор, залуп, манда, гондон, шлюха, сука), vulgar words just as out of place on a dealer's frame or badge (грёбаный, жопа, говно, дерьмо, срать, ссать, хер, дрочить, пердеть, трахать, сиськи, шалава, мразь, ублюдок, чмо) and a few English ones (fuck, cunt, shit, bitch…), seen through disguises: case, ё, Latin and digit look-alikes (a, c, e, o, p, x, y, k, m, h, t, b→в; 0, 1, 3, 4, 6, @), letters spaced, dotted or hyphenated apart, a star for a letter. Roots that occur inside ordinary words are anchored so хлеб, небо, себе, вебинар, рубля, гребля, гребень, мандарин, команда, мудрый, страхуем, гондола, сравнение, сруб, ссылка, ссуда, SsangYong, Херсон, херес, страх, трахея and Scunthorpe pass. Jokes and plain insults («Шляпа полная», дурак, лох, задница) are deliberately not caught. Client-side only, no library: extend the lists in `profanity.ts` and check ordinary words against them.

## Links with settings

The price card, livery and plate frame generators keep their settings in the page address, so a set-up card or livery can be sent as a link: «Копировать» in the sidebar copies it, as does the address bar; it's off (#999 text) while «Скачать» is, as a link is sent for a finished result, not a draft («Сбросить» stays) (the livery's «Сбросить» isn't shown while everything is at its defaults, «Копировать» staying; the plate frame's «Копировать» and «Сбросить» aren't shown while there's no dealer and the line is on the left: nothing to copy or reset; they were off, #999 text, before); «Сбросить» clears it back to the defaults, keeping the model. The page reads them on load and writes them back with `history.replaceState` as they change; only what differs from the defaults goes in, and anything unknown or implausible falls back to the default. Messengers may encode a pasted link again (Telegram turns `%0A` into `%250A`), so `linkParams` decodes escapes left after decoding, up to twice. Price card: `model` (umo8; UMO 5 is the default in both models' generators and comes first in the switch, as in Figma 5015:11121 — it was UMO 8, so links from then without `model` open UMO 5), `trim`, `full` (seven digits, else the trim's price), `credit` (the credit price is on: `auto` follows the full price, seven digits are set by hand), `link` (left out while it's the model's own page); these are the first card's, the next cards' carry their number (`model2`, `full3`, `link2`) and `cards` says how many there are (up to 20; left out for one). Livery: `model` (umo8), `link`, `top`, `bottom` (the texts, line breaks as `%0A`; `bottom` is left out while it's the model's default), `off` (any of `qr,top,bottom,rdealer,rslogan`; `rdealer` and `rslogan` leave the dealer or the slogan off the rear window), `rear=on` (the rear window, off by default; links from when it was on by default open without it, and their `off=rear` is ignored), `rbottom` (the rear window's slogan once it differs from the sides'; `rtop`, its own dealer name, is no longer read, so such links open with the sides' name), `show` (`dims,seams`), `size=large` (the 60 mm bottom text) and `qr=small` (the small QR on the sides). Plate frame: `name` (the dealer's name), `text` (the whole line, which also turns the hidden free mode on; the first links carried the line this way, so they open in it), `align=center`. The name tag generator keeps nothing in the address and has no «Копировать»: a staff list isn't sent as a link. Adding a setting means reading it in the page's initial state and writing it in its `useLinkState` call; keep the names stable, as links get sent around.

## Car photos

The car photos shown on the price card live at fixed paths so they can be swapped without touching code:

- `src/assets/umo5-car.jpg` — UMO 5
- `src/assets/umo8-car.jpg` — UMO 8

The exported PDF is printed at A3, and the car photo is meant to hold up at 300dpi — don't shrink it down to "web size." Target **3840×2160 for UMO5** and **3520×1980 for UMO8** (native resolution the car image container needs at 300dpi on an A3 sheet). Export as JPEG quality ~90; that lands each file around 1.5-2 MB. To update a photo, replace the file in place (keep the same filename).

CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) and the deploy workflow both fail the build if any image under `src/` or `public/` exceeds 2.5 MB (and any `.mp4`/`.webm` 15 MB) — high enough for a real 300dpi print asset, low enough to catch a mistake like the raw 13 MB PNG that used to ship here and silently failed to load for users on slow connections.

## Code quality

- Use double quotes for strings containing apostrophes (`"We're here to help"`), or escape them in single-quoted strings. An unescaped apostrophe in a single-quoted string breaks the build.
- Ensure JSX tags are closed and braces are balanced.
- Export components as default exports.
