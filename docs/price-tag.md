# Price tag (`/price-tag`)

A price tag for the small goods at a dealership (Figma: UMO | Evrone, node 4202:4368; the Word template it replaces is in Yandex Disk `02 UMO/Price Tag`), printed on an office printer and cut out with scissors: 90×60 mm, numbers in `src/pricetag/tag.ts` (10 Figma px = 1 mm). A black band 12 mm tall: the UMO logo 15×3 at 4.5, 4.5, a 0.15 mm white hairline at 22.5 and the dealer's name in CoFo Sans Medium 4.4 mm (case forms on) 2.625 mm after it, one line free to run to the tag's right edge (64.875 mm, so the longest dealers fit). On white, within 4.5 mm sides: the goods' name in Medium 4.5 mm on 4.5 mm lines from 16 mm, up to four lines within 81 mm, a word too long for a line broken after its hyphen or slash, else between letters (`splitWord`); the code (optional) and the caption («Цена за штуку с НДС» by default; a combobox also offering «за комплект», «за упаковку») in Regular 3 mm with 1% tracking; the price in Medium 9 mm, lining tabular figures (`lnum`, `tnum`, substituted by hand), set in threes with «₽», 9 999 999,99 at most («Цена больше 9 999 999,99»), kopecks after a comma when there are any («1 290,50 ₽»; «,00» left out). The price field shows the kopecks it doesn't have yet in grey after the typed price (`PriceField`: a copy of the text laid under the field, transparent but for the tail), so kopecks never have to be typed; a comma or dot starts them; the value is one string, `formatPrice` setting it as typed, `priceText` as printed. The layout is fixed: code, caption and price stand where they are however long the name. Figma's boxes are «leading-none», so a baseline is 0.844 of the size under a box's top (`DROP`).

The dealer is set once for the list, the first field, above «Данные». The caption and the price share a row, half each, where the form is 400 px or wider (a container query), one under the other otherwise; a longer caption is cut to one line with an ellipsis (`truncate` on `ComboField`). The template `public/downloads/UMO_price-tags_template.xlsx` (Наименование, Артикул, Цена, Подпись; the price as a number, read with its kopecks from a number or text cell by `readPrice`). Name, price and dealer are required.

`UMO_price-tags.pdf` is A4 sheets, eight tags 2×4 at 1:1 butting each other, the block centred (15 mm at the sides, 28.5 at the top and bottom), text and logos in outlines, 100% K; 0.25 pt cut lines in K25 run across the whole sheet at every row's and column's edge, the middle one white over the bands; on a sheet not full they stop under the last row.

«Скачать Word» is commented out in `PriceTag.tsx` (CoFo Sans shows only in Word for Windows, and the layout breaks easily in Word). Uncommented, it goes in the bar's `links` with «Поделиться» (a fragment): `UMO_price-tags.docx`, the brand team's template (`src/assets/pricetag/UMO_price-tag_template.docx`, CoFo Sans embedded) with the texts put in (`tagsDocx`): its first tag row (five table rows: band, name, code, caption, price, 60 mm) is the pattern, its sample texts («GAC Левобережный», «Яндекс Станция Макс…», «YNDX-00053», «Цена за штуку с НДС», «30 000 ») replaced cell by cell, so keep them in the template if it's redrawn; a table per eight tags, in the flow, centred, 19,4 mm from the top, a page break between, grey cut lines round every tag. The band's logo picture is 3 mm tall on the baseline, the name 12.5 pt with 3 mm capitals (`band`), 2.625 mm after the hairline, the picture cropped by its last 0.375 mm and the band cells' right margin 0.

Both files come back (`readTagsFile`): the PDF carries the dealer and goods in its info (see «Files dropped back» in `docs/generators.md`), the Word file is read off its table, five rows a tag. Any other PDF or Word file is said to have no tags; an .xlsx is a table. `useStaff` takes `accept`, `uploadLabel` and `countWord` for this («3 ценника · заменить»). Not kept in the address, but a link brings the dealer and goods, and with `pdf` opens the PDF (see «Links with settings» in `docs/generators.md`); the PDF's title is its file name.

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in the tag's column, `UMO_price-tags_template.pdf`: that tag alone, in the sheet's first place, the text live in CoFo Sans, the dealer white on the band, logos and cut lines in outlines (`tagsTemplate`); `umo.autos/price-tag?t` gives a sheet of eight placeholder tags (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
