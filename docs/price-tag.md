# Price tag (`/price-tag`)

A price tag for the small goods at a point, printed on an office printer and cut out with scissors: laid out by the brand (`BrandPriceTag` in `src/pricetag/tag.ts`, its numbers in the brand's `price-tag.ts` and `README.md`; 10 Figma px = 1 mm). A black band: the brand's logo, a white hairline and the point's name in the brand font Medium (case forms on), one line free to run to the tag's right edge, so the longest points fit. On white, within the sides: the goods' name in Medium on lines as tall as its size, up to the brand's number of lines, a word too long for a line broken after its hyphen or slash, else between letters (`splitWord`); the code (optional) and the caption («Цена за штуку с НДС» by default; a combobox also offering «за комплект», «за упаковку») in Regular with tracking; the price in Medium, lining tabular figures (`lnum`, `tnum`, substituted by hand), set in threes with «₽», 9 999 999,99 at most («Цена больше 9 999 999,99»), kopecks after a comma when there are any («1 290,50 ₽»; «,00» left out). The price field shows the kopecks it doesn't have yet in grey after the typed price (`PriceField`: a copy of the text laid under the field, transparent but for the tail), so kopecks never have to be typed; a comma or dot starts them; the value is one string, `formatPrice` setting it as typed, `priceText` as printed. The layout is fixed: code, caption and price stand where they are however long the name. Figma's boxes are «leading-none», so a baseline is `DROP` of the size under a box's top, from the font's own measures.

The point is set once for the list, the first field, above «Данные». The caption and the price share a row, half each, where the form is 400 px or wider (a container query), one under the other otherwise; a longer caption is cut to one line with an ellipsis (`truncate` on `ComboField`). The template `brand/<name>/public/downloads/<Brand>_price-tags_template.xlsx` (Наименование, Артикул, Цена, Подпись; the price as a number, read with its kopecks from a number or text cell by `readPrice`). Name, price and point are required.

`<Brand>_price-tags.pdf` is A4 sheets, eight tags 2×4 at 1:1 butting each other, the block centred (15 mm at the sides, 28.5 at the top and bottom), text and logos in outlines, 100% K; 0.25 pt cut lines in K25 run across the whole sheet at every row's and column's edge, the middle one white over the bands; on a sheet not full they stop under the last row.

The PDF comes back (`readTagsFile`): it carries the point and goods in its info (see «Files dropped back» in `docs/generators.md`). Any other PDF is said to have no tags; an .xlsx is a table. `useStaff` takes `accept`, `uploadLabel` and `countWord` for this («3 ценника · заменить»). Not kept in the address, but a link brings the point and goods, and with `pdf` opens the PDF (see «Links with settings» in `docs/generators.md`); the PDF's title is its file name.

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in the tag's column, `<Brand>_price-tags_template.pdf`: that tag alone, in the sheet's first place, the text live in the brand font, the point white on the band, logos and cut lines in outlines (`tagsTemplate`); `<site>/price-tag?t` gives a sheet of eight placeholder tags (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
