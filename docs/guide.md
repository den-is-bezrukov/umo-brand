# Brand guide (`/`)

`src/guide/Guide.tsx` is the web version of the UMO brand guide page from Figma ([UMO | Evrone, node 4810:686](https://www.figma.com/design/i6PdEDo72Vab0cAixG0nIz/UMO-%257C-Evrone?node-id=4810-686)). Text and layout are live code; every illustration is a Figma frame exported at 2x and saved as `src/assets/guide/<name>.webp` (cwebp `-q 90`), picked up automatically by `import.meta.glob`.

To refresh an illustration, re-export that frame at 2x and overwrite the file with the same name. Export the whole section Block and crop the frame out of it: frames that stretch inside a grid/flex parent come out of Figma's direct export at the wrong size (a mostly empty canvas).

Vector schemes (clear space, minimum size, co-branding, type specimens, model lettering) are SVG files in the same folder — `img()` prefers `<name>.svg` over `<name>.webp`. They are cut out of Figma SVG exports of the parent Body nodes by `scripts/split-figma-svg.py` (node ids and crop boxes at the bottom of the script). Text inside them is outlined by Figma. The lettering pictures (`lettering-model8` / `lettering-model5`: MODEL lettering over its number plate) are laid out by the script's `assemble()` from separate layer exports, as those frames export stretched. Photos, logo misuse examples, app/social icons and model posters stay WebP.

## Structure and rhythm

Each chapter is a `Chapter` (id, title, then its `Section`s). Chapters stay two levels deep — chapter, then its sections. A section's opening picture goes above its heading, with the section anchor on the picture. Anchor ids sit on an empty marker above the title, so keep new chapters inside `Chapter` rather than a bare `<h2 id>`. Links to the guide have gone out, so when an anchor id changes, add the old one to `OLD_ANCHORS` in `Guide.tsx`: it is redirected to the new id on load and on a hash change.

Body copy (`Text`) takes its width from its length on the 12-column Figma grid: up to 150 characters 6 columns (432px), up to 300 — 8 (600px), longer — 9 (678px). Don't set widths by hand (the one exception, `wide`, gives Контакты 9 columns); texts paired side by side sit in a grid with `*:max-w-none` and fill their cells.

Vertical rhythm is fluid: `--spacing-section` (96px on a 375px phone → 216px at the 1440px Figma frame) and `--spacing-chapter` (144 → 288px) in `src/index.css`, used as `gap-section` between sections and as the extra top padding of a `Chapter`; don't hard-code breakpoint gaps between blocks.

Below 520 px landscape photos — raster `Fig`s 2:1 and wider (`isWidePhoto`) — are cropped to 4:3 with `object-cover`; `focus` sets the crop's horizontal point in % where the subject isn't central (`umo5-kv`: 72). An object on a flat background (`flat`, its colour: the livery, #f6f6f6) is fitted into the 4:3 frame whole instead. SVG schemes stay whole; the Логотип plate and the clear-space scheme alone are 4:3 on phones (`phone43`), the scheme fitted whole on its grey. From 520 px some pairs already stand side by side: the logo-on-icons pictures, Леттеринг → Модели (its downloads from 560, `early` on `Assets`) and Сделано в Москве. Below `md` pictures stand 16 px apart, as the page's side gutter, across and down, and so do a picture and the download rows under it (24 from `md`).

## First screen

The first screen follows Figma 4893:3846 and 4897:4565 (prototype 4921:2352). On `lg`+ the hero (`Hero`) is fixed full-width behind the page, which starts a strip above the bottom of the screen (`--strip`, set by `useHeroStrip`), so the first screen shows the photo, the logo (the UMO × Яндекс lockup, see below), the «☰ Содержание» row, the statement and the quick links under it: Платформа бренда → `#positioning` (landing its picture 24px under the top of the screen from `lg` — `lg:scroll-mt-6`), Материалы → `#materials`, Редактор → `/editor`, from `lg` only (below it they're in the bottom bar), the guide with a window of every constructor over it (`EditorDialog`, titled «Редактор материалов»: centred, 360 wide, corners 16px round, the guide dimmed at 40% as under the phone's contents sheet; the title in 16 Medium with a cross, the constructors 24 Medium 20 apart in alphabetical order; the dim, the cross or Esc close it, back where you came from, or to `/` when opened by its link; below `lg` the same list comes out of the bottom bar in its sheet instead — the contents' sheet, titled «Редактор материалов», the bar saying «Свернуть», pulled, tapped or Esc'd away as the contents are — `sheetEditor`); no link to the official site, 48px below the statement; scrolling, the page slides up over the photo. The sidebar's toggle row is fixed to the bottom of the screen in CSS, not script (moved by script it lagged the scroll a frame). Until the page docks at the top the contents fade in over the first 160px of scroll; the row does what it says anywhere, on the first screen also scrolling the page up to the top; on the first screen, where the contents are under half faded in, it shows the menu bars even while the list is expanded, and a click takes the page up with the list open. The photo stays still (no parallax). «Платформа бренда» lands the page's top edge at the top of the screen (`lg:scroll-mt-[calc(24px-0.164em)]` on the statement). The statement is aligned optically by CoFo Sans's metrics: the body's 16px on top plus `lg:mt-[calc(8px-0.164em+var(--umo-y-overhang))]` put its capitals level with the top of UMO at any size, and the strip is sized so the quick links share a line with the toggle row, however many lines the statement wraps to.

A scroll that stops between the photo and the docked page (below `lg`, the header reaching the top) is carried on to the end it was heading for, however short (`useHeroSnap`, `HERO_SNAP_MIN` = 0): it waits for the scroll and its inertia to end and a finger to let go; not with reduced motion. The logo takes you to the very top (`toTop`): from further than two screens down it first jumps to where the page has just docked (a screen down below `lg`) and glides only the rest, rather than flying through the whole guide.

**The UMO × Яндекс logo** (Figma 5156:543, `src/guide/UmoYLogo.tsx`; `COBRAND` there turns it off in one place: UMO alone, no extra room). On the first screen it's the lockup, UMO | Я; as the page docks it folds into UMO — the cobrand slides into the O's right wall, the Я turns a quarter and shrinks behind the cobrand's clip, the divider steps aside — linearly with the scroll. From `lg` UMO starts as high as the statement's capitals (0.68 em of CoFo Sans, or lower where the lockup wouldn't fit the sidebar) and ends 24 high (120 wide) in the corner; below `lg` it stays 24 high. It's built of HTML layers (wordmark, cobrand clip, divider, Я) whose transforms run as CSS scroll-driven animations (`src/index.css`, over `--umo-y-fold`, the page's rise) — moving parts inside one SVG redrew it on the main thread and lagged a finger on iPhone; the sidebar's lockup is drawn at its first-screen size (`--u`) and scaled down, and its moves, which depend on the screen, are written as keyframes by `useHeroReveal`. Without scroll timelines (Firefox) a script makes the same moves. The Я's circle overhangs UMO by a third of its height, so on the first screen the sidebar's logo, contents and toggle row stand 36px from the edges (`UMO_Y_PAD` 12 over the usual 24), the statement 12px lower to keep its capitals level with UMO and the quick links 12px higher on the row's line, going back to 24 as the page docks (transforms too); below `lg` 8px of white stand between the film and the header (`UMO_Y_GAP`), scrolling away with it.

Below `lg` the film is the first thing on the page, above the header, and takes what the first screen has left above the header, statement and the bottom bar with a gap over it (`--mstrip`, measured by `useHeroStrip`), so the first section starts below the fold. Where that would leave it less than half the screen's width or 40% of its height (a phone held sideways, a short window), it takes the whole first screen down to the bar instead (`data-hero-full`), so the bar never cuts the statement. The quick links stand in the bottom bar after the bare menu icon at any width («Платформа» without «бренда» under 420px, where the three and the icon wouldn't fit), as far from it as from each other (16px, 24 from `md`); each takes taps over the bar's whole height and half the gap to its neighbours, so a tap a little off the word doesn't open the contents, spaced as the bar's edges (16px, 24 from `md`), instead of their own line under the statement, and stand there until the first section's heading is lit, when the section's name takes their place at once (`BarLinks`; no fade).

The hero is the UMO test-drive film (source `02 UMO/Site Video/Test Drive/` on Yandex Disk), looped and muted: `src/assets/guide/hero.mp4` is `UMO_test-drive_1080.mp4` byte for byte (H.264, 12 MB) and `hero-720.mp4` is `UMO_test-drive_720.mp4` (1280×720, 6.2 MB), both already fast-start and silent — don't re-encode them — with their first frame as `hero-poster.webp`. The 1080p plays by default and the 720p on a slow connection: at once where the browser reports the speed (Network Information API: `effectiveType` 3G or slower; not `downlink`, which Chrome reads far too low at first), and in any browser when the 1080p hasn't buffered enough to play 3 s after the page opens. It plays only while some of it is on screen; with reduced motion or data saving on, the still `hero.webp` stands in. CI and the deploy fail on any video over 15 MB.

## Navigation

Navigation follows Figma section TOC (node 4865:1079). On `lg`+ the sidebar is 320 px, from `xl` a third of the screen and the page the other two thirds, with no cap. The hero isn't in the list: the logo leads there. The sidebar lists chapters with only the one you're reading open; the space between items is the links' own padding (chapters 8px above and under, items 4px, the last item of a chapter 8px under it), so the whole list is clickable (Figma 4865:1031), and the list's -8px top and bottom margins tuck the first and last chapters' padding under the logo and the toggle row (4865:1025); the «☰ Содержание» row (`TocToggle`; not «Меню», which repeats the icon: the menu bars spread every chapter, and the row turns into «Свернуть» with its chevrons, Figma 5030:11292, `src/icons/toc.ts`) is pinned to the bottom of the screen. Above the first chapter Платформа бренда stands open and lit. The choice is remembered in `localStorage`. When the list is taller than the screen, the sidebar scrolls itself to keep the item you're reading in view (`useActiveInView`).

Below `lg` the header holds just the logo; a bar fixed at the bottom shows a menu icon (two bars 6 px apart, `src/icons/toc.ts`; the generators' menu has the same) and the title of the heading you're reading (any level, from `NAV`), the menu icon alone on the first screen with the quick links after it (on any width; «Платформа» without «бренда» under 420px), as the desktop row, and opens the full, expanded contents as a sheet of a fixed height, ten item rows (28px each, the list's 16px over them; 24 from `md`), 60% of the screen at most, its top corners 16 px round, over the page dimmed at 40% (a tap on it closes it), the list scrolling inside it and opened at the chapter you're reading, its title at the top as the first chapter's stands (its block at the sheet's top edge, so the chapter before, its stuck title included, is gone whole; the last chapters, which can't rise that far, leave the list at its end, moved so no chapter start falls under the stuck title's 44px, and the list ends with 44px of room for that) (the item you're at in the middle only where the chapter runs past the sheet before it). There's one bar: the sheet comes out from behind it and goes back there, and the bar turns into «Свернуть» (a second «Свернуть» row riding on the sheet read as a duplicate menu). The sheet follows a finger (`useTocSheet`, as the generators' sheet): the bar pulled up opens it, pulled down closes it, as does the list pulled down while scrolled to its top; let go, it goes on in 350 ms, slowing down, to the nearer of open and closed, giving at 30% past open; taps, Esc and links slide it too. In the sheet a chapter's title stays at the top of the list while its items scroll by (`STICKY_CHAPTER`), covering its own 8px over the text and 4px under it (an item's padding), so the descenders of the item scrolling under it don't peek out. Chapter titles in the sheet stand 2 px in from the list's edge (their items 22 px further), and the bullet of the one you're reading 12 px left of its text, about midway between it and the screen's edge. The bar's white runs on past the screen's bottom (a white border as tall as the bar, and a screen of white under it, both overlapping by 2px), as the header's does upwards: iOS 26 Safari draws the page under its floating toolbar, and while that slides the page showed through. Header, contents and bar keep 24px padding down to `md` and 16px below it.

Links have two hover styles: the contents in the sidebar and the mobile menu turn grey #808080 (`NAV_HOVER`); links in the page — the quick links, download rows and captions — get a hairline underline as set in Figma 4865:1061: black at 40%, 2.5% of the type size thick, 25% under the baseline, no skip ink, fading out over 0.25s (`UNDERLINE`, `LINK_HOVER`). A link inside body copy (`TEXT_LINK`) keeps that underline at 40% so it reads as a link, and turns black on hover.

Plain "logo on a flat colour" figures are not images: `LogoPlate` renders `src/guide/UmoLogo.tsx` (inline SVG, `currentColor`) centred on a background, with the logo width in Figma frame units.

## Downloads

Downloads live in `public/downloads/` and are listed by the `Assets` component: one row per file (↓, file name, size) or per page of the site (↗, title, path), the arrow 8 px off the name. Some pictures do what a row under them does (`PreviewLink` inside a `group/preview`, with that row passed to `Assets` as `preview`): the logo, the favicon and the UMO | Яндекс example download their SVG, the Ливрея and Прайс-карта previews (`Constructor`) open the constructor; pointing at the picture underlines its row. Where pictures lead to different rows, the section is a `Previews` group, where a pointed picture lights its own row.

Under Логотип на иконках, after the favicon, `umo-icon-black.zip` (white logo on black) and `umo-icon-white.zip` (black on white), each holding the icon as SVG (720 grid) and PNG (1024×1024): a square tile with no rounding, so any platform can crop it, the logo 2/3 of its width and centred. The pictures stand userpic and favicon on light over app icon and favicon on dark, two columns from 520 px; the app icon picture downloads the black one, the userpic the white one, the favicon pictures the favicon. `umo-favicon.svg` / `.ico` are copies of `public/favicon.svg` / `.ico`: update them together.

1–3 rows stay in one column, 4+ split into two filled top to bottom, so list files grouped by item. Sizes come from the `virtual:download-sizes` module (`downloadSizesPlugin` in `vite.config.ts`), in КБ with a decimal comma; every row of a list gets one decimal as soon as one size is under 10, none otherwise. Each item ships as `<file>.svg` (black) and `<file>-png.zip` (black + white transparent PNGs). `umo-logo` (2400×480) sits under the Логотип figure, `umo-yandex` (the UMO | Яндекс lockup, 2080×400) under Кобрендинг, `umo-model-8` / `umo-model-5` (MODEL 8 / MODEL 5 lettering, 2400×280, exported from the `Vector` layers of Figma frame 4818:1961) under Леттеринг → Модели. The number plates `umo-plate-8` / `umo-plate-5` (5200×1120, the 520×112 mm plate) come in two colours that are separate designs, so they ship as one `<file>-svg.zip` holding `-black` (white on black) and `-white` (black on white), with no PNGs; the sources are `UMO-<n>_plate_b/_w.svg` from the Nameplate folder. UMO 8 files come first so they fill the left column. Keep hairlines as filled rects, not stroked zero-width paths — ImageMagick drops the latter when rasterising. If the logo changes, update `umo-logo.svg` and `UmoLogo.tsx` together, then regenerate the PNGs, e.g. `magick -background none -density 360 umo-logo.svg -resize 2400x480 PNG32:umo-logo-black.png` (swap `fill="black"` for `white` for the white one), and re-zip with `zip -X -j`.

## Favicon and site icons

Icons in `public/` (Figma fav 4870:3556): `favicon.svg` and `favicon.ico` are the U module of the logo, white on a black tile (the full wordmark turns to mush at 16px). The tile is rounded in the icon itself: radius 1 on the 16 grid with 60% corner smoothing, since Safari and Chrome show favicons as drawn. The U is hinted: on a 16-unit grid its stems (2), bar (2) and edges sit on whole units, so it lands on whole pixels at 16, 32 and 48; the .ico's 16px frame keeps the hand-drawn U with mirrored corners and takes its rounded-corner alpha from the SVG; 32/48 are Chrome renders of the SVG at 768px, box-downscaled. `apple-touch-icon.png` (180) carries the whole UMO wordmark at 2/3 of the tile (120×24 at 30,78), as the app icon in Логотип на иконках.

## The guide in UMO Sans (`/umo-sans`)

`src/guide/SansGuide.tsx` is the brand guide set in UMO Sans instead of CoFo Sans, to judge the font in place: it wraps `Guide` and sets `--font-sans`, so live text changes and pictures don't. Not linked from anywhere. The font is `fonts/umo-sans/` (sources, build, README); its Regular and Medium WOFF2 are copied to `public/fonts/UMOSans-*.woff2` and declared in `src/index.css` — copy them again after rebuilding the font.
