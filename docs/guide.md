# Brand guide (`/`)

The web version of a brand's guide page from its Figma, in three parts: the page (`src/guide/Guide.tsx`: the first screen, the contents, Материалы, the editor's window, Контакты), the blocks its sections are written with (`src/guide/parts.tsx`: `Chapter`, `Section`, `Text`, `Fig`, `Assets`, `Swatch`, `Dictionary`…), and the brand's guide itself, `brand/<name>/guide/index.tsx`: its chapters in the contents (`CHAPTERS`), what they say (`Chapters`), the first screen's film and statement, the pictures of Материалы (`DRAWN`), Контакты' text and the footer. Text and layout are live code; every illustration is a Figma frame exported at 2x and saved as `brand/<name>/guide/images/<name>.webp` (cwebp `-q 90`), picked up automatically by `import.meta.glob`.

To refresh an illustration, re-export that frame at 2x and overwrite the file with the same name. Export the whole section Block and crop the frame out of it: frames that stretch inside a grid/flex parent come out of Figma's direct export at the wrong size (a mostly empty canvas).

Vector schemes (clear space, minimum size, co-branding, type specimens, model lettering) are SVG files in the same folder — `img()` prefers `<name>.svg` over `<name>.webp`. They are cut out of Figma SVG exports of the parent Body nodes by `scripts/split-figma-svg.py` (how: the `figma-to-guide` skill). Text inside them is outlined by Figma. The lettering pictures (`lettering-model8` / `lettering-model5`: MODEL lettering over its number plate) were laid out by the script's `assemble()` from separate layer exports, as those frames export stretched. Photos, logo misuse examples, app/social icons and model posters stay WebP.

## Structure and rhythm

Each chapter is a `Chapter` (id, title, then its `Section`s). Chapters stay two levels deep — chapter, then its sections. A section's opening picture goes above its heading, with the section anchor on the picture. Anchor ids sit on an empty marker above the title, so keep new chapters inside `Chapter` rather than a bare `<h2 id>`. Links to the guide have gone out, so when an anchor id changes, add the old one to `OLD_ANCHORS` in `Guide.tsx`: it is redirected to the new id on load and on a hash change.

Body copy (`Text`) takes its width from its length on the 12-column Figma grid: up to 150 characters 6 columns (432px), up to 300 — 8 (600px), longer — 9 (678px). Don't set widths by hand (the one exception, `wide`, gives Контакты 9 columns); texts paired side by side sit in a grid with `*:max-w-none` and fill their cells.

Vertical rhythm is fluid: `--spacing-section` (96px on a 375px phone → 216px at the 1440px Figma frame) and `--spacing-chapter` (144 → 288px) in `src/index.css`, used as `gap-section` between sections and as the extra top padding of a `Chapter`; don't hard-code breakpoint gaps between blocks.

Below 520 px landscape photos — raster `Fig`s 2:1 and wider (`isWidePhoto`) — are cropped to 4:3 with `object-cover`; `focus` sets the crop's horizontal point in % where the subject isn't central (72 for a subject on the right). An object on a flat background (`flat`, its colour: the livery, #f6f6f6) is fitted into the 4:3 frame whole instead. SVG schemes stay whole; the Логотип plate and the clear-space scheme alone are 4:3 on phones (`phone43`), the scheme fitted whole on its grey. From 520 px some pairs already stand side by side: the logo-on-icons pictures, Леттеринг → Модели (its downloads from 560, `early` on `Assets`) and Сделано в Москве. Below `md` pictures stand 16 px apart, as the page's side gutter, across and down, and so do a picture and the download rows under it (24 from `md`).

## Chapters

- Материалы (anchor `materials`; they were Носители, renamed for the dealers' word, «рекламные материалы», the quick link Материалы leading here) holds the templated media: a two-column intro (its first sentence lists every generator from `src/data/generators.ts`, each as its `phrase` linked to it, in `INTRO_ORDER`, a new one after them before «и») and a grid of the media (`Carriers`), with no section per medium: each card is the medium's picture right over its ↗ row to the constructor (no hairline over the row, unlike the asset lists), one row under it: what a dealer does there («Указать часы работы», `row`), the row opening its constructor, and the generator's `?t` file a PDF link on its right, in a new tab; pointed at, the card (picture or row) shows ↗ after the name, 8 px off, and the PDF ↓ there instead, the font's arrows, the row's verb giving way to the template's name meanwhile («Шаблон визитки», its `label` in `src/data/generators.ts`) (the template; the price card's four default cards, the livery's; `templates` in `src/data/generators.ts`) — not the medium's name with «Редактор» beside it; they were two rows, the constructor's and «Шаблон режимника» (`row` in `DRAWN`, also the picture's `aria-label`, the cards with pictures made for them; a generator in `src/data/generators.ts` that `DRAWN` doesn't have gets a 444×333 card of its own after them, its `row` from that list and its picture drawn by its generator (`GENERATED`, lazy: the memo's two pages, `src/memo/MemoPreview.tsx`, loading as the card nears the screen) or grey, until a picture is made and the card moves up into `DRAWN`; the last of them goes wide, 912×456, when it would stand alone in its row (an odd number of narrow cards; not a row of three, its cards a third smaller than the rest), narrow again once another generator pairs it), and carries its anchor (`livery`, `price-card`, `price-tag`, `business-card`, `name-tag`, `hours-sign`, `plate-frame`, the sidebar's sub-items); pictures 2:1 and wider span the width, the rest pair up from 640 px, a narrow one moving up next to an unpaired one. The media are one list, `MEDIA_LIST`; `packMedia` turns it into the order the cards stand in (`MEDIA`), and both the grid and the sidebar's sub-items follow it. A new medium is one entry there. The Ливрея card shows `brand/<name>/guide/images/livery-<model>.webp` (2x), Визитка `business-card-card.webp` (2x), Бейдж `name-tag-card.webp` (4:3, with its colour settings and crop baked in; `name-tag.webp`, the plain photo, is the maker's reference in the badge archive), Рамка номера is 4:3 everywhere: the plate in its frame, 261×66, centred on the grey. Ценник and Режимник have no Figma frame: they are drawn live from the constructors' numbers (`src/pricetag/tag.ts`, `src/hours/sign.ts`) in SVG with the brand font text, the 90×60 tag as wide as the plate (58.8%), the 480×680 sign as tall as the price card (47.3% wide), on the same grey.
- Контакты (`contact`, last) stands above the footer; the two take at least two thirds of the screen from the chapter's anchor down, so the short chapter's heading reaches the upper third and gets lit in the contents; the line a heading is lit at slides from a third of the screen down to two thirds over the last screen of scroll (`useActiveSection`). Its contact is the support bot, the link on the words «напишите боту поддержки» (no handle, Telegram not named) to the brand's bot (`SITE.support`); the text is the brand's (`Contact`).

## First screen

On `lg`+ the hero (`Hero`) is fixed full-width behind the page, which starts a strip above the bottom of the screen (`--strip`, set by `useHeroStrip`), so the first screen shows the photo, the logo (see below), the «☰ Содержание» row, the statement and the quick links under it: Платформа бренда → `#brand`, the chapter's title, Материалы → `#materials` (the templates are there only; not in the phone bar under 380px, where the three don't fit), Редактор → `/editor` (the window is «Редактор материалов»; «Редактор» alone after Материалы, with no echo; Материалы was out of the quick links for a while), from `lg` only (below it they're in the bottom bar), the guide with a window of every constructor over it (`EditorDialog`; the list is `GENERATORS` in `src/data/generators.ts`, as the generators' ring, not the guide's grid, so a generator shows there before its picture is in the grid; titled «Редактор материалов», in the site's one dialog `src/ui/Dialog.tsx`, which the generators' «Инструкция» uses too: centred across and down, at least 64px off the screen's edges, 360 wide, corners 16px round, the guide dimmed at 40% as under the phone's contents sheet; the title in 16 Medium with the generators' cross (`CrossIcon`) at 2 px, the constructors 24 Medium 20 apart in alphabetical order, each opening in a new tab, the guide staying, and pointed at getting the page links' hairline underline and a ↗ after it, 8 px off (`EditorLinks`; they turned grey); the dim, the cross or Esc close it, back where you came from, or to `/` when opened by its link; below `lg` the same list comes out of the bottom bar in its sheet instead — the contents' sheet, titled «Редактор материалов», as tall as the constructors in it (not the contents' eleven rows), the bar saying «Свернуть», pulled, tapped or Esc'd away as the contents are — `sheetEditor`); no link to the official site, 48px below the statement; scrolling, the page slides up over the photo. The sidebar's toggle row is fixed to the bottom of the screen in CSS, not script (moved by script it lagged the scroll a frame). Until the page docks at the top the contents fade in over the first 160px of scroll; the row does what it says anywhere, on the first screen also scrolling the page up to the top; on the first screen, where the contents are under half faded in, it shows the menu bars even while the list is expanded, and a click takes the page up with the list open. The photo stays still (no parallax). The statement is aligned optically by the brand font's metrics: the body's 16px on top plus `lg:mt-[calc(8px-0.164em+var(--logo-overhang))]` put its capitals level with the top of the wordmark at any size, and the strip is sized so the quick links share a line with the toggle row, however many lines the statement wraps to.

A scroll that stops between the photo and the docked page (below `lg`, the header reaching the top) is carried on to the end it was heading for, however short (`useHeroSnap`, `HERO_SNAP_MIN` = 0): it waits for the scroll and its inertia to end and a finger to let go; not with reduced motion. The logo takes you to the very top (`toTop`): from further than two screens down it first jumps to where the page has just docked (a screen down below `lg`) and glides only the rest, rather than flying through the whole guide.

**The header's logo** is the brand's (`brand/<name>/HeaderLogo.tsx`, with `COBRAND` and `setLogoFold`; how the brand's folds is in its `README.md`). The page folds it with the scroll over the film's height (`--logo-fold`), and from `lg` brings the sidebar in with it (`logo-desk`, `-nav`, `-toggle` in `src/index.css`, written by Guide for the screen); without scroll timelines Guide's script makes the same moves (`setLogoFold`). From `lg` the logo starts as high as the statement's capitals (`LOGO_HEIGHT`, in the statement's em) and ends 24 high (120 wide) in the corner; below `lg` it stays 24 high. With a cobrand the first screen keeps room for the partner's mark (`LOGO_PAD`, `LOGO_GAP`).

Below `lg` the film is the first thing on the page, above the header, and takes what the first screen has left above the header, statement and the bottom bar with a gap over it (`--mstrip`, measured by `useHeroStrip`), so the first section starts below the fold. Where that would leave it less than half the screen's width or 40% of its height (a phone held sideways, a short window), it takes the whole first screen down to the bar instead (`data-hero-full`), so the bar never cuts the statement. The quick links stand in the bottom bar after the bare menu icon at any width, by their full names (a trial); where they run past the bar it scrolls sideways to the screen's edge, 8px after it, as any label stands after its icon, 16px apart (24 from `md`); each takes taps over the bar's whole height and half the gap to its neighbours, so a tap a little off the word doesn't open the contents, spaced as the bar's edges (16px, 24 from `md`), instead of their own line under the statement, and stand there until the first section's heading is lit, when the section's name takes their place at once (`BarLinks`; no fade).

The hero is the brand's film, looped and muted: `brand/<name>/guide/images/hero.mp4` (1080p) and `hero-720.mp4`, both fast-start and silent as they came — don't re-encode them — with their first frame as `hero-poster.webp`. The 1080p plays by default and the 720p on a slow connection: at once where the browser reports the speed (Network Information API: `effectiveType` 3G or slower; not `downlink`, which Chrome reads far too low at first), and in any browser when the 1080p hasn't buffered enough to play 3 s after the page opens. It plays only while some of it is on screen; with reduced motion or data saving on, the still `hero.webp` stands in. CI and the deploy fail on any video over 15 MB.

## Navigation

On `lg`+ the sidebar is 320 px, from `xl` a third of the screen and the page the other two thirds, with no cap. The hero isn't in the list: the logo leads there. The sidebar lists chapters with only the one you're reading open; the space between items is the links' own padding (chapters 8px above and under, items 4px, the last item of a chapter 8px under it), so the whole list is clickable, and the list's -8px top and bottom margins tuck the first and last chapters' padding under the logo and the toggle row; the «☰ Содержание» row (`TocToggle`; not «Меню», which repeats the icon: the menu bars spread every chapter, and the row turns into «Свернуть» with its chevrons, `src/icons/toc.ts`) is pinned to the bottom of the screen. Above the first chapter Платформа бренда stands open and lit. The choice is remembered in `localStorage`. When the list is taller than the screen, the sidebar scrolls itself to keep the item you're reading in view (`useActiveInView`).

Below `lg` the header holds just the logo; a bar fixed at the bottom shows a menu icon (two bars 6 px apart, `src/icons/toc.ts`; the generators' menu has the same) and the title of the heading you're reading (any level, from `NAV`), the menu icon alone on the first screen with the quick links 8px after it (on any width, scrolling sideways where they don't fit), as the desktop row, and opens the full, expanded contents as a sheet 60% of the screen over the bar, cut down to whole rows (every row in it, chapter or item, 36px for a finger; the list's 16px over them, 24 from `md`; `--toc-h`), its top corners 16 px round, over the page dimmed at 40% (a tap on it closes it), the list scrolling inside it and opened at the chapter you're reading, its title at the top as the first chapter's stands (the list stops only where a chapter's title is stuck just where it stands anyway, its block 8px above the edge, or where an item stands whole right under a stuck title, so no row is cut in half and no title of the chapter before peeks; where the chapter can't rise to the top, or the item you're at would be under the edge, at the first such place with that item as the last row, the list getting the few pixels of room under it that takes) (the item you're at in the middle only where the chapter runs past the sheet before it). There's one bar: the sheet comes out from behind it and goes back there, and the bar turns into «Свернуть» (a second «Свернуть» row riding on the sheet read as a duplicate menu). The sheet follows a finger (`useTocSheet`, as the generators' sheet): the bar pulled up opens it, pulled down closes it, as does the list pulled down while scrolled to its top; let go, it goes on in 350 ms, slowing down, to the nearer of open and closed, giving at 30% past open; taps, Esc and links slide it too. In the sheet a chapter's title stays at the top of the list while its items scroll by (`STICKY_CHAPTER`), covering its own 8px over the text and 4px under it (an item's padding), so the descenders of the item scrolling under it don't peek out. Chapter titles in the sheet stand 2 px in from the list's edge (their items 22 px further), and the bullet of the one you're reading 12 px left of its text, about midway between it and the screen's edge. The bar's white runs on past the screen's bottom (a white border as tall as the bar, and a screen of white under it, both overlapping by 2px), as the header's does upwards: iOS 26 Safari draws the page under its floating toolbar, and while that slides the page showed through. Header, contents and bar keep 24px padding down to `md` and 16px below it.

Links have two hover styles: the contents in the sidebar and the mobile menu turn grey #808080 (`NAV_HOVER`); links in the page — the quick links, download rows and captions — get a hairline underline: black at 40%, 2.5% of the type size thick, 25% under the baseline, no skip ink, fading out over 0.25s (`UNDERLINE`, `LINK_HOVER`). A link inside body copy (`TEXT_LINK`) keeps that underline at 40% so it reads as a link, and turns black on hover.

Plain "logo on a flat colour" figures are not images: `LogoPlate` renders `brand/<name>/Wordmark.tsx` (inline SVG, `currentColor`) centred on a background, with the logo width in Figma frame units.

## Downloads

Downloads live in `brand/<name>/public/downloads/` and are listed by the `Assets` component: one row per file (its name in Russian, `label`, and its format on the right, `formatOf`: the extension, or what an archive holds, «logo-png.zip» PNG; not the file's name, which a dealer doesn't read, nor its size, every file here being small; one item in two formats is one row, `also`: the row downloads the vector, the PNG (the favicon's ICO) after its SVG is a link of its own, as tall as the row, pointing at it lighting it alone; they were a row each); the name and a format underlined only while pointed at themselves, the name also while its picture is (the row or picture pointed at shows the arrow and turns the main format black) or per page of the site (title, path); ↓ after the name, 8 px off, while the row, a format of it or its picture is pointed at (`named`; ↗ in Материалы over the card); a file's link titled «Скачать SVG» (its format; a tooltip, nothing beside it), a generator's «Редактировать визитку» (`editTitle`, from its `one`), so are the pictures, the intro's links and the editor window's; no ↓ or ↗ before the name (`ROW_ARROWS`, off as a trial: the right column says it already), the arrow 8 px off the name when on. Some pictures do what a row under them does (`PreviewLink` inside a `group/preview`, with that row passed to `Assets` as `preview`): the logo, the favicon and the co-branding example download their SVG, the Ливрея and Прайс-карта previews (`Constructor`) open the constructor; pointing at the picture underlines its row. Where pictures lead to different rows, the section is a `Previews` group, where a pointed picture lights its own row.

1–3 rows stay in one column, 4+ split into two filled top to bottom, so list files grouped by item. Each item ships as `<file>.svg` (black) and `<file>-png.zip` (black + white transparent PNGs). Keep hairlines as filled rects, not stroked zero-width paths — ImageMagick drops the latter when rasterising.

## Favicon and site icons

Icons in `brand/<name>/public/`: `favicon.svg` and `favicon.ico` (a hinted 16/32/48 set, so it lands on whole pixels) and `apple-touch-icon.png` (180); the `favicon-kit` skill makes and checks them. How the brand's are drawn is in its `README.md`.
