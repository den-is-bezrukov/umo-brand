# Brand guide (`/`)

`src/guide/Guide.tsx` is the web version of the UMO brand guide page from Figma ([UMO | Evrone, node 4810:686](https://www.figma.com/design/i6PdEDo72Vab0cAixG0nIz/UMO-%257C-Evrone?node-id=4810-686)). Text and layout are live code; every illustration is a Figma frame exported at 2x and saved as `src/assets/guide/<name>.webp` (cwebp `-q 90`), picked up automatically by `import.meta.glob`.

To refresh an illustration, re-export that frame at 2x and overwrite the file with the same name. Export the whole section Block and crop the frame out of it: frames that stretch inside a grid/flex parent come out of Figma's direct export at the wrong size (a mostly empty canvas).

Vector schemes (clear space, minimum size, co-branding, type specimens, model lettering) are SVG files in the same folder — `img()` prefers `<name>.svg` over `<name>.webp`. They are cut out of Figma SVG exports of the parent Body nodes by `scripts/split-figma-svg.py` (node ids and crop boxes at the bottom of the script). Text inside them is outlined by Figma. The lettering pictures (`lettering-model8` / `lettering-model5`: MODEL lettering over its number plate) are laid out by the script's `assemble()` from separate layer exports, as those frames export stretched. Photos, logo misuse examples, app/social icons and model posters stay WebP.

## Structure and rhythm

Each chapter is a `Chapter` (id, title, then its `Section`s). Chapters stay two levels deep — chapter, then its sections. A section's opening picture goes above its heading, with the section anchor on the picture. Anchor ids sit on an empty marker above the title, so keep new chapters inside `Chapter` rather than a bare `<h2 id>`. Links to the guide have gone out, so when an anchor id changes, add the old one to `OLD_ANCHORS` in `Guide.tsx`: it is redirected to the new id on load and on a hash change.

Body copy (`Text`) takes its width from its length on the 12-column Figma grid: up to 150 characters 6 columns (432px), up to 300 — 8 (600px), longer — 9 (678px). Don't set widths by hand (the one exception, `wide`, gives Контакты 9 columns); texts paired side by side sit in a grid with `*:max-w-none` and fill their cells.

Vertical rhythm is fluid: `--spacing-section` (96px on a 375px phone → 216px at the 1440px Figma frame) and `--spacing-chapter` (144 → 288px) in `src/index.css`, used as `gap-section` between sections and as the extra top padding of a `Chapter`; don't hard-code breakpoint gaps between blocks.

Below 520 px landscape photos — raster `Fig`s 2:1 and wider (`isWidePhoto`) — are cropped to 4:3 with `object-cover`; `focus` sets the crop's horizontal point in % where the subject isn't central (`umo5-kv`: 72). An object on a flat background (`flat`, its colour: the livery, #f6f6f6) is fitted into the 4:3 frame whole instead. SVG schemes stay whole; the Логотип plate and the clear-space scheme alone are 4:3 on phones (`phone43`), the scheme fitted whole on its grey. From 520 px some pairs already stand side by side: the logo-on-icons pictures, Леттеринг → Модели (its downloads from 560, `early` on `Assets`) and Сделано в Москве. Below `md` pictures stand 16 px apart, as the page's side gutter, across and down, and so do a picture and the download rows under it (24 from `md`).

## Chapters

- Платформа бренда (positioning, vision, mission, audience, voice) has no title of its own: the statement under the hero («Автомобильный бренд, созданный в технологическом партнёрстве с Яндексом»; from `lg` 60px in the 912px Figma column, scaled with the column below that — `min(60px, 6.58cqw)` — so it wraps the same three lines at any width) stands in for it and carries the `brand` anchor, so it isn't a `Chapter`.
- Фотография (anchor `photography`, after Цвет) opens with its two principles side by side over the `keyvisual` picture, then UMO 5 and UMO 8 (`photo-umo5`, `photo-umo8`): each model's photo with its ad banners, no caption. The banners are `Poster` components: a photo-only background (`<model>-<banner|square>-bg.webp`, about 3x the Figma frame) with the title, subtitle and `UmoYandexLockup` laid over it in container-width units, so text and lockup stay sharp at any size. The backgrounds were cut from the source photos using the layer geometry in Figma (the direct export of those layers is broken the same way as stretched frames).
- Кобрендинг (Figma 4818:1328) is two columns from 640 px (`sm`), a scheme over its example, on 4:3 cards: the four 444×333 Figma cards exported as SVG (see the note in `scripts/split-figma-svg.py`: they export 684 wide, viewBox shifted by 120). The schemes open large on a click (`Zoom`, a lightbox: a click anywhere or Esc closes it, the page doesn't scroll under it).
- Типографика: the CoFo Sans specimen is 4:3 below 520 px (2:1 from it); below 640 px its alternatives stand two to a row, 4:3, three in a row from `sm`.
- Голос → Примеры (`examples`, Figma 5030:11343) shows the voice as the brand's own messages (`Examples`): chat-like grey bubbles (`Bubble`: #f5f5f5, 16 px round but the bottom-left corner, title 20 medium over 16 text) and photos with a white line over them, shaped as the bubbles (`PhotoCard`; a line alone is at most 240 wide, so it breaks the same on any card; `voice-alice`, `voice-runner`, `voice-charging`.webp, exported at 2x with their text removed, as the text is live). Three columns from `sm`, each a stack; the middle and right photos take what their column has left (`grow`), so the columns end level. «Запоминающиеся фары» and the runner's «…город комфортнее» are kept apart from the typograph's tie of the last word. On phones one column (the middle column's bubble after its runner photo), photos cropped to 4:3, the runner's at 20% from the top (`focus`). The Alice mark in «Электромобиль с @ Алисой…» is «@» set in Alice Okniks, Denis's own icon font (local repo `~/Documents/GitHub/alice-okniks`, not on GitHub; its README says how to use it), shipped as `public/fonts/AliceOkniks.woff` (from `dist/Alice Okniks.ttf`; WOFF, as brotli for WOFF2 isn't installed) and declared in `src/index.css`; Medium with the axes at 0 and `ss02` (Drop) on, which lowers the mark by 18 to sit with CoFo Sans. After a rebuild of the font, convert it again.
- Цвет (anchor `color`, after Леттеринг, no sections) states that the brand has two colours, black and white, the colour coming from the car and photography, and that the greys are service colours. The colours are `Swatch` tiles, 4:3: black and white (444×333) over the three greys (288×216); below 640 px the greys stand two to a row, 16 px inside, label and code at least 4 px apart; from 520 px to `sm` white joins them as a small tile, two by two under the black; narrower it stays large. Each tile carries its values in the guide's 16px type: the name at the top in body copy size (18, 20 from `md`), at the bottom a row per value, a short label and the value; a row with a code (HEX, CMYK, K) is a button as a whole, underlined on hover, copying only the code (`CopyRow`) and saying «Скопировано» in the label's place for 0,5 s. Black is #000000 on screen, C60 M40 Y40 K100 for film and signs (the livery's and name tag's colour) and K100 for text (the business card's); white #FFFFFF, paper on light and white ink on dark; Фон #F5F5F5 (K4), Линии #E6E6E6 (K10), Подписи #808080 (K50), the greys the guide itself uses — all its secondary text is #808080. The red and yellow plates under Цвет логотипа are captioned as examples only, not brand colours. The logo misuse examples (Ограничения) stand two to a row on phones, three from 640 px, 16 px apart below md.
- Пространства holds the brand's physical places (its intro with the dealership photo; the Дилерский центр section is drafted and commented out in `Guide.tsx`).
- Носители (anchor `materials`) holds the templated media: a two-column intro and a grid of the media (`Carriers`, Figma 4844:6695), with no section per medium: each card is the medium's picture right over its ↗ row to the constructor (no hairline over the row, unlike the asset lists), named for what a dealer does there — «Настроить ливрею», «Обновить прайс-карту», «Проставить цены», «Сделать визитки», «Подписать бейджи», «Указать часы работы», «Сделать рамку номера» — its path on the right (`row` in `MEDIA_LIST`), and carries its anchor (`livery`, `price-card`, `price-tag`, `business-card`, `name-tag`, `hours-sign`, `plate-frame`, the sidebar's sub-items); pictures 2:1 and wider span the width, the rest pair up from 640 px, a narrow one moving up next to an unpaired one. The media are one list, `MEDIA_LIST`; `packMedia` turns it into the order the cards stand in (`MEDIA`), and both the grid and the sidebar's sub-items follow it. A new medium is one entry there. The Ливрея card shows `src/assets/guide/livery-umo5.webp` (Figma 4985:10090 at 2x), Визитка `business-card-card.webp` (5008:10428 at 2x), Бейдж `name-tag-card.webp` (4:3, from 4985:10051 with its colour settings and crop baked in; `name-tag.webp`, the plain photo, is the maker's reference in the badge archive), Рамка номера is 4:3 everywhere (Figma 5008:10482): the plate in its frame, 261×66, centred on the grey. Ценник and Режимник have no Figma frame: they are drawn live from the constructors' numbers (`src/pricetag/tag.ts`, `src/hours/sign.ts`) in SVG with CoFo Sans text, the 90×60 tag as wide as the plate (58.8%), the 480×680 sign as tall as the price card (47.3% wide), on the same grey.
- Контакты (`contact`, last) stands above the footer; the two take at least two thirds of the screen from the chapter's anchor down, so the short chapter's heading reaches the upper third and gets lit in the contents; the line a heading is lit at slides from a third of the screen down to two thirds over the last screen of scroll (`useActiveSection`). Its contact is the support bot, the link on the words «напишите боту поддержки» (no handle, Telegram not named) to [@umo_support_bot](https://t.me/umo_support_bot); it ends «— разберёмся вместе», then, a paragraph of its own, «Спасибо, что делаете UMO вместе с нами».

## First screen

The first screen follows Figma 4893:3846 and 4897:4565 (prototype 4921:2352). On `lg`+ the hero (`Hero`) is fixed full-width behind the page, which starts a strip above the bottom of the screen (`--strip`, set by `useHeroStrip`: 286px at 1440), so the first screen shows the photo, the 200px logo, the «Содержание» row, the statement and the quick links under it (Платформа → `#positioning`, landing its picture 24px under the top of the screen from `lg` — `lg:scroll-mt-6`; Материалы для дилеров → `/ai`, the page that makes them with Alice or in the editor (it was `#materials`); no link to the official site), 48px below the statement; scrolling, the page slides up over the photo. The sidebar's toggle row is fixed to the bottom of the screen in CSS, not script (moved by script it lagged the scroll a frame). Until the page docks at the top, `useHeroReveal` fades the contents in over the first 160px of scroll; the photo stays still (no parallax), the logo shrinks with the scroll from 200px wide to its usual 120 by the time the page docks, and the row does what it says anywhere, on the first screen also scrolling the page up to the top; on the first screen, where the contents are under half faded in, it says «Содержание» even while the list is expanded, and a click takes the page up with the list open. The logo scrolls smoothly to the very top (`toTop`). «Платформа бренда» lands the page's top edge at the top of the screen (`lg:scroll-mt-[calc(24px-0.164em)]` on the statement). The statement is aligned optically by CoFo Sans's metrics: the body's 16px on top plus `lg:mt-[calc(8px-0.164em)]` put its capitals level with the top of the logo at any size, and the strip is sized so the quick links share a line with the toggle row, however many lines the statement wraps to.

Below `lg` the film is the first thing on the page, above the header, and takes what the first screen has left above the header, statement, quick links and the bottom bar with a gap over it (`--mstrip`, measured by `useHeroStrip`), so the first section starts below the fold; it is never less than half the screen's width.

The hero is the UMO test-drive film (source `02 UMO/Site Video/Test Drive/` on Yandex Disk), looped and muted: `src/assets/guide/hero.mp4` is `UMO_test-drive_1080.mp4` byte for byte (H.264, 12 MB) and `hero-720.mp4` is `UMO_test-drive_720.mp4` (1280×720, 6.2 MB), both already fast-start and silent — don't re-encode them — with their first frame as `hero-poster.webp`. The 1080p plays by default and the 720p on a slow connection: at once where the browser reports the speed (Network Information API: `effectiveType` 3G or slower; not `downlink`, which Chrome reads far too low at first), and in any browser when the 1080p hasn't buffered enough to play 3 s after the page opens. It plays only while some of it is on screen; with reduced motion or data saving on, the still `hero.webp` stands in. CI and the deploy fail on any video over 15 MB.

## Navigation

Navigation follows Figma section TOC (node 4865:1079). On `lg`+ the sidebar is 320 px, from `xl` a third of the screen and the page the other two thirds, with no cap. The hero isn't in the list: the logo leads there. The sidebar lists chapters with only the one you're reading open; the space between items is the links' own padding (chapters 8px above and under, items 4px, the last item of a chapter 8px under it), so the whole list is clickable (Figma 4865:1031), and the list's -8px top and bottom margins tuck the first and last chapters' padding under the logo and the toggle row (4865:1025); the «Содержание / Свернуть» row (`TocToggle`, the menu icon and the «Свернуть» chevrons, Figma 5030:11292, from `src/icons/toc.ts`) is pinned to the bottom of the screen. The choice is remembered in `localStorage`. When the list is taller than the screen, the sidebar scrolls itself to keep the item you're reading in view (`useActiveInView`).

Below `lg` the header holds just the logo; a bar fixed at the bottom shows a menu icon and the title of the heading you're reading (any level, from `NAV`), «Содержание» on the first screen, and opens the full, expanded contents as a sheet from the bottom, 60% of the screen at most, its top corners 16 px round, over the page dimmed at 40% (a tap on it closes it), the list scrolling inside it and opened at the chapter you're reading, with «Свернуть» at the foot. Header, contents and bar keep 24px padding down to `md` and 16px below it.

Links have two hover styles: the contents in the sidebar and the mobile menu turn grey #808080 (`NAV_HOVER`); links in the page — the quick links, download rows and captions — get a hairline underline as set in Figma 4865:1061: black at 40%, 2.5% of the type size thick, 25% under the baseline, no skip ink, fading out over 0.25s (`UNDERLINE`, `LINK_HOVER`). A link inside body copy (`TEXT_LINK`) keeps that underline at 40% so it reads as a link, and turns black on hover.

Plain "logo on a flat colour" figures are not images: `LogoPlate` renders `src/guide/UmoLogo.tsx` (inline SVG, `currentColor`) centred on a background, with the logo width in Figma frame units.

## Downloads

Downloads live in `public/downloads/` and are listed by the `Assets` component: one row per file (↓, file name, size) or per page of the site (↗, title, path), the arrow 8 px off the name. Some pictures do what a row under them does (`PreviewLink` inside a `group/preview`, with that row passed to `Assets` as `preview`): the logo, the favicon and the UMO | Яндекс example download their SVG, the Ливрея and Прайс-карта previews (`Constructor`) open the constructor; pointing at the picture underlines its row. Where pictures lead to different rows, the section is a `Previews` group, where a pointed picture lights its own row.

Under Логотип на иконках, after the favicon, `umo-icon-black.zip` (white logo on black) and `umo-icon-white.zip` (black on white), each holding the icon as SVG (720 grid) and PNG (1024×1024): a square tile with no rounding, so any platform can crop it, the logo 2/3 of its width and centred. The pictures stand userpic and favicon on light over app icon and favicon on dark, two columns from 520 px; the app icon picture downloads the black one, the userpic the white one, the favicon pictures the favicon. `umo-favicon.svg` / `.ico` are copies of `public/favicon.svg` / `.ico`: update them together.

1–3 rows stay in one column, 4+ split into two filled top to bottom, so list files grouped by item. Sizes come from the `virtual:download-sizes` module (`downloadSizesPlugin` in `vite.config.ts`), in КБ with a decimal comma; every row of a list gets one decimal as soon as one size is under 10, none otherwise. Each item ships as `<file>.svg` (black) and `<file>-png.zip` (black + white transparent PNGs). `umo-logo` (2400×480) sits under the Логотип figure, `umo-yandex` (the UMO | Яндекс lockup, 2080×400) under Кобрендинг, `umo-model-8` / `umo-model-5` (MODEL 8 / MODEL 5 lettering, 2400×280, exported from the `Vector` layers of Figma frame 4818:1961) under Леттеринг → Модели. The number plates `umo-plate-8` / `umo-plate-5` (5200×1120, the 520×112 mm plate) come in two colours that are separate designs, so they ship as one `<file>-svg.zip` holding `-black` (white on black) and `-white` (black on white), with no PNGs; the sources are `UMO-<n>_plate_b/_w.svg` from the Nameplate folder. UMO 8 files come first so they fill the left column. Keep hairlines as filled rects, not stroked zero-width paths — ImageMagick drops the latter when rasterising. If the logo changes, update `umo-logo.svg` and `UmoLogo.tsx` together, then regenerate the PNGs, e.g. `magick -background none -density 360 umo-logo.svg -resize 2400x480 PNG32:umo-logo-black.png` (swap `fill="black"` for `white` for the white one), and re-zip with `zip -X -j`.

## Favicon and site icons

Icons in `public/` (Figma fav 4870:3556): `favicon.svg` and `favicon.ico` are the U module of the logo, white on a black tile (the full wordmark turns to mush at 16px). The tile is rounded in the icon itself: radius 1 on the 16 grid with 60% corner smoothing, since Safari and Chrome show favicons as drawn. The U is hinted: on a 16-unit grid its stems (2), bar (2) and edges sit on whole units, so it lands on whole pixels at 16, 32 and 48; the .ico's 16px frame keeps the hand-drawn U with mirrored corners and takes its rounded-corner alpha from the SVG; 32/48 are Chrome renders of the SVG at 768px, box-downscaled. `apple-touch-icon.png` (180) carries the whole UMO wordmark at 2/3 of the tile (120×24 at 30,78), as the app icon in Логотип на иконках.

## The guide in UMO Sans (`/umo-sans`)

`src/guide/SansGuide.tsx` is the brand guide set in UMO Sans instead of CoFo Sans, to judge the font in place: it wraps `Guide` and sets `--font-sans`, so live text changes and pictures don't. Not linked from anywhere. The font is `fonts/umo-sans/` (sources, build, README); its Regular and Medium WOFF2 are copied to `public/fonts/UMOSans-*.woff2` and declared in `src/index.css` — copy them again after rebuilding the font.
