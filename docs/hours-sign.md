# Hours sign (`/hours-sign`)

The dealership's opening hours plate (Figma: UMO | Evrone, node 3819:4211; the source in Yandex Disk `02 UMO/Hours Sign`, `UMO_hours-sign.pdf`, 480×680 mm, Illustrator's large canvas at 1:10), numbers in `src/hours/sign.ts` (Figma's frame is 2400×3400, 5 px a millimetre; within half a millimetre of the source). The margins and column are sevenths of the width (68,57 and 342,86 mm), the UMO logo across the column from the top margin. The hours centred, CoFo Sans Medium 58,5 mm, −0,85% tracking, lining tabular figures and case forms («9:00—21:00», the dash raised), always as wide as Figma's «9:00—21:00» at its size, so a longer range is smaller and a shorter one larger, on the same baseline. Each time is a field with − and + either side, half an hour a click or ↑ ↓, typed through a mask (`maskTime`: hours one digit from 3 on, two up to 24, the minutes' first digit 0–5, anything else dropped), the rest of «9:00» grey after what's typed, rounded to the half hour when left. Closing can't be at or before opening: a time stepped past the other, or typed past it once left, pushes the other along half an hour apart (`commitFrom`, `commitTo`), within 0:00–24:00 (no dealer works overnight). A day under 8 hours is said under the sign in red, «День короче 8 часов — проверьте время», a warning that leaves the download on. The line under them, 31,1 mm, −0,8%, is fixed as «Работаем без выходных» (its combobox of `HOURS_LINES` is commented out; `line` and `off=line` in a link are ignored). The dealership at the bottom, Medium 9,14 mm on 1,5 lines, standing on its last line: the marketing name («UMO » fixed), «Адрес: …» (up to two lines, balanced: after the comma leaving the lines nearest in length unless every comma leaves the longer line more than 15% of the column longer than it need be, then wherever they come nearest; never right after «Адрес:»; `balanced`), «Телефон: …», the site.

Only one range of hours: the six dealers open different hours on different days get none filled in. The dealer combobox puts in its umo.auto address and hours and its showroom's phone (`phoneOf`) (`hoursOf`: «ежедневно, 09:00–21:00» → 9:00 and 21:00) while they're empty or the previous dealer's, and the site follows the name (`siteFor`) until edited; the address is a combobox of the dealer's own address, then every dealer's; the phone is the business card's mask. Every field is required, grey on the sign as its placeholder until filled in. `UMO_hours-sign.pdf`: the plate at 1:1, no bleed, logo and text as filled outlines in C60 M40 Y40 K100; dropped back it opens its fields (another file is «В файле нет режимника»).

The PDF's title is its file name. A link with `pdf` saves `UMO_hours-sign.pdf` to the downloads and opens it with no editor; a sign at fault opens the editor (see «Links with settings» in `docs/generators.md`).
