# Hours sign (`/hours-sign`)

The opening hours sign, laid out as the brand's `hours-sign.ts` says (`BrandHoursSign`, the mechanics in `src/hours/sign.ts`): the sheet, the form's fields in order after the hours (`point`, `address`, `phone`, `site`, `text`, each with its label, placeholder, the download button's ask and a default), and one or more variants (more: «Фон» at the end of the form, kept in the link as `variant` and in the PDF's data), each a list of blocks: the logo; the hours («9:00—21:00», or «10–19» with `minutes: 'unlessZero'`, kept as wide as `fit` or shrunk to the column); a line or several (`lines`), centred by the ink or from the left; columns of rows hanging from a first baseline or standing on the last, a row of more lines than one breaking between words, two balanced. A block's text is a template of the fields: `Адрес: {address}` keeps «Адрес: » fixed, `{legal:title}` sets a company's name as a title (`titleOf`: no quotes, the legal form on a line of its own). A field with a default starts with it and the reset brings it back; the link leaves out a field equal to its default. A black variant is rich black with the text white. A brand's `hours.slider` gives one slider with two knobs instead (`HoursSlider`): half an hour a step over 0:00–24:00, the day between them filled in `accent`, their times over the track, a knob stopping half an hour short of the other, ← → by the keys; each knob a range of its own over one track (`.hours-range` in `src/index.css`). Otherwise each time is a field with − and + either side, half an hour a click or ↑ ↓, typed through a mask (`maskTime`: hours one digit from 3 on, two up to 24, the minutes' first digit 0–5, anything else dropped), the rest of «9:00» grey after what's typed, rounded to the half hour when left. Closing can't be at or before opening: a time stepped past the other, or typed past it once left, pushes the other along half an hour apart (`commitFrom`, `commitTo`), within 0:00–24:00 (no point works overnight). A day under 8 hours is said under the sign in red, «День короче 8 часов — проверьте время», a warning that leaves the download on. Two lines are balanced (`balanced`): after the comma leaving the lines nearest in length unless every comma leaves the longer line more than 15% of the column longer than it need be, then wherever they come nearest; never right after the first word, a prefix («Адрес:»).

Only one range of hours: the points open different hours on different days get none filled in. The point combobox puts in its the brand's site address and hours and its showroom's phone (`phoneOf`) (`hoursOf`: a point's `hours`, «9:00–21:00», → 9:00 and 21:00; UMO's points turn umo.auto's «ежедневно, 09:00–21:00» into it) while they're empty or the previous point's, and the site follows the name (`siteFor`) until edited; the address is a plain field, as the business card's, the point's address put in as it's picked and edited by hand (not a list of every point's address, which let a picked point take another's); the phone is the business card's mask. Every field is required, grey on the sign as its placeholder until filled in. `<Brand>_hours-sign.pdf`: the sign at 1:1, no bleed, logo and text as filled outlines in the brand's print black (`ink` in its `colors.ts`); dropped back it opens its fields (another file is «В файле нет режимника»).

The PDF's title is its file name. A link with `pdf` saves `<Brand>_hours-sign.pdf` to the downloads and opens it with no editor; a sign at fault opens the editor (see «Links with settings» in `docs/generators.md`).

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in the caption before its reset, `<Brand>_hours-sign_template.pdf`: the same page, the text live in the brand font Medium in the brand's print black (`ink` in its `colors.ts`), the logo in outlines (`signTemplate`; the lines noted by `placeRuns`); `<site>/hours-sign?t` gives it blank, every field its placeholder and the fixed lines as they are (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
