# Business card (`/business-card`)

A dealership employee's business card, 90×50 mm, numbers in `src/card/card.ts` (10 Figma px = 1 mm; Figma trims text boxes to the capitals, 680 of 1000 units). The face is the logo 40×8 mm in the bottom left corner. The back, within 5 mm margins: the dealership at the top, hanging from the top margin — its name in the brand font Medium 4 mm (case forms on), then «Официальный дилер <Бренд>», the address (up to two lines) and the site in Regular 3 mm, within 65 mm but the address, which runs under the logo to its right edge (80 mm), the logo 15×3 in the top right corner level with the name's capitals — and the person at the bottom, standing on the bottom margin — name and surname on one line of Medium 4 mm within 60 mm (never shrunk), then position (up to two lines, breaking where typed or as `POSITIONS` breaks it), email and phone in Regular 3 mm, the QR 15×15 in the bottom right corner. 2.3 mm between the capitals under a heading, 2 mm between items, 100% leading inside one; a line left out closes up towards its edge. The position is picked from the name tag's list on one line (`POSITION_OPTIONS`) and broken where the list breaks it (`withListBreak` in `buildBack`), or where typed.

The dealership (picked from `DEALER_NAMES` without the brand's name, as the logo stands by it; it puts in its the brand's site address while the address is empty or the previous dealer's), the site and the QR are set once for the whole list. The address, the site and the QR's link sit under «Редактировать» at the end of the «Дилер» label row («Свернуть» closes them); the download's lead opens them when one is missing. The site follows the dealership's name until edited: its the brand's site subdomain, the name transliterated (`siteFor`: «Дилер Город» → dealer-city.<brand site>; such subdomains didn't resolve as of 2026-10-06). The QR is the site until the link is edited.

Picking the dealer puts its showroom's phone (`phoneOf`) into every person's phone that's empty or the previous dealer's, and a person added starts with it (still fresh), as the salesman's line is often the salon's number and his extension; a link's person with no phone takes it too. The phone field is a live mask (`maskPhone`): digits only, set as «+7 987 654 32 10» while typed (its placeholder), a first 8 becoming +7 (8 800 keeping its 8), a number without a code getting +7, ten digits after it at most, the caret staying after the same digit. The card sets it as «+7 495 000 00 00» however it came in (`formatPhone`), a city with a four-digit code as «+7 4922 77 07 36» (`phoneGroups`: three digits for mobiles, 8 800 and the cities with seven-digit numbers, `SEVEN_DIGIT_CITIES`, four for the rest; the mask groups the same way as it goes, the hours sign's too; anything not a Russian number is «Телефон: проверьте номер»). The extension, the one optional field, is the number's second part (one grey field under one label, each part 8 px round): «Доб.» grey in it, then up to six tabular digits, shown on the card as «доб. 12345»; a number pasted with its extension («… доб. 204», «ext», «#», a comma) splits into both (`splitPhone`). The email field suggests, through a native `<datalist>`, what's typed before the @ at the card's site (`mailDomain`), then at `MAIL_DOMAINS` (yandex.ru, mail.ru, gmail.com, ya.ru, bk.ru, inbox.ru, list.ru, rambler.ru). Any address with an @ and a dotted domain passes, Cyrillic ones too, unless a part of it (before the @, a domain label) mixes Latin and Cyrillic letters, as a Russian «о» in «ivanоv» does: «Почта: смешаны русские и латинские буквы». Every other field is required and shows as a grey placeholder on the card, named as its field; a grey code stands in for the QR until there's a link.

The template is `brand/<name>/public/downloads/<Brand>_business-cards_template.xlsx` (Имя, Фамилия, Должность, Почта, Телефон, Добавочный; the last three as text, so Excel keeps «+7»). `<Brand>_business-cards.pdf`: each page the card at 1:1, 90×50, no bleed, text and logos in outlines, 100% K. Two checkboxes at the foot of the form: «Обложка у всех» (from two cards on) puts the face before every back instead of once before them all, in the file only — the preview keeps one face at the top; «Метки реза» adds crop marks, the page then 96×56 with the card 3 mm in (its TrimBox), the marks 0,25 pt in registration black from 1 mm off the card's edges to the page's. The face has no number. Not kept in the address, but one person's card can come in by a link, read on load only (`fromLink`): `dealer` (its the brand's site address and site follow, as when picked; `address`, `site`, `qr` override them; `marks` turns crop marks on), `name`, `surname`, `position`, `phone`, `ext`, `email`; `pdf` then opens the PDF itself with no editor, a card at fault opening the editor instead.

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in the card's column, `<Brand>_business-cards_template.pdf`: the face and that card's back (the grey sample QR while there's no link), with «Обложка у всех» and «Метки реза» as set, the text live in the brand font, logos and QR in outlines (`cardsTemplate`); `<site>/business-card?t` gives a card of placeholders (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
