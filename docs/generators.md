# Generators: what they share

## Generator form (`src/ui/form.tsx`)

The sidebar is 320 px plus a 1 px stroke on wide screens (Figma 4844:6865 price card, 4900:4588 livery).

**Header** (`GeneratorHeader`, Figma 5098:5487, the same on every screen): the breadcrumbs «Бренд UMO» → `/` (the way back to the guide; UMO's logo would lead to umo.auto) · «Редактор материалов», the app itself, named as the guide's quick link, which shows every generator (no «Материалы» crumb between them; no chevron after it, which only cluttered the row), in Medium on 16 px lines (no menu icon by them: the generators are an app of their own, where a burger promised the whole app's menu and opened only the generators' list). Under them the generators' line (`GeneratorRing`, on `Ring` in `src/ui/Ring.tsx`): scrolled natively by a finger, trackpad or wheel, and picked as a drum — the name the line comes to rest on, 24 px from the edge, black as it goes, opens the instant the finger is off and the line still (`scrollend`; 150 ms with no scrolling where a browser lacks it), a tap opening one too. It goes round: the generators (`GENERATORS`, alphabetical: Бейдж, Визитка, Ливрея, Прайс-карта, Рамка номера, Режимник, Ценник) three times over, the middle copy's current one at the edge, so there's a whole turn to scroll either way; each page puts its own there. On the keyboard the line is one Tab stop, ringed whole (its names take no Tab stops): ← → turn it without opening anything, Enter or Space opens the name at the edge (the next page taking the focus back on its line), Esc or the focus leaving turns it home. Not opened by the arrows themselves: one stray press left a page and its unsaved list (the name tag's, business card's and price tag's are kept nowhere), as macOS and Figma never switch on an arrow alone. 24 px Medium, others #BFBFBF, #808080 on hover, 20 px apart. The crumb opens them in place (Figma 5103:6083), nothing in the row moving, and the line, the rest of the form and the download give way to every generator, 20 px under the crumbs as the line's names stand, 24 px Medium 16 px apart, the current one black (`html[data-menu]` in `src/index.css`); the crumb again, the current name or Esc fold it. The same header, not a second sheet over the form.

**Инструкция** (`HelpButton` in `src/ui/help.tsx`, as Composter's «Помощь»): a «?» (the glyph itself, in the crumbs' type, #808080, black on hover and while open) at the end of the breadcrumbs row opens «Инструкция» in the site's one dialog (`src/ui/Dialog.tsx`, the guide's «Редактор материалов» window: over black at 40%, centred across and 64 px from the top (16 on phones), growing with its text with no scroll inside (the dark scrolls when it's taller than the screen), 16 px round, 24 px in, the title in 16 px Medium with × (the generators' `CrossIcon` at 2 px, as the chevron, `src/ui/icons.tsx`); 560 px wide here; Esc, × or a click on the dark close it; portalled, so the phone's sheet doesn't hold it). Titled with what's being made, «Как сделать рамку номера» and, 8 px after it, a combobox's chevron (`ChevronIcon`, `src/ui/icons.tsx`), «Общие вопросы» for what they share, opened on the page's own. The made thing and the chevron open the list of instructions in the dialog's body, in place of the text, as «Редактор материалов» in the header opens the generators (24 px Medium, 16 apart, the open one black, the rest #bfbfbf): the generators in the ring's order and «Общие вопросы»; a pick shows that instruction from its top, the title again or Esc fold the list back (tried: a ring of tabs under the title, a combobox's list over the text, «Все инструкции» at the foot). A generator's section: one line on what it makes, «Шаги» (numbered), «Из таблицы» (name tag, business card, price tag), «Печать» (whom to send the file and what to say, or how to print it) and «Вопросы» about it; «Общее» holds the questions they share (the black button, a dealer not in the list, «Поделиться», mending a file, phones). The texts are in `HELP` and `GENERAL` there; keep them in step when a generator's fields, button names or files change. Metrika goal `open_help`. The generators' own controls still carry no hint texts: help lives only here.

**Controls**: segments, fields, the QR link field, a combobox (`ComboField`: a text field with suggestions in a popover under it, narrowed as you type, and by the same text read in the other keyboard layout, key for key, after what's found as typed: «fufn» finds АГАТ, «vtytl» Менеджер, `otherLayout` in `src/ui/keys.ts`; what's typed stays until a suggestion is picked), checkboxes (2 px tick, 8 px to the label, 4 px round), the size switch («Крупнее» / «Мельче» with arrows out or in). Styled per Figma 5015:11121: 8 px corners on segments, fields, buttons and the suggestions list; segments, tabs and sections have no hover, the picked segment a white tile edged at 10% on the grey and the others' text #808080; a focused field edged at 40%; a field with an error is never edged in red, its label turns red instead (`ALERT_LABEL`: the label of a `Field`, an `OptionalField`'s checkbox or the business card's «Дилер» finds the control's aria-invalid under it with `group-has`); the keyboard's focus one look on every page and every control but fields, 2 px at 30% black, 2 px off, round with the element's corners (one unlayered `:focus-visible` rule in `src/index.css`, no focus classes on the controls; the checkbox draws it on its box), fields keeping their focused edge; icons a 2 px stroke with square ends and bevelled corners; row words (the size switch, «Редактировать») and quiet lines #808080, black on hover; secondary buttons («Поделиться») edged at 10%, at 40% on hover; the suggestions list white, 2 px inside its edge, with a soft shadow, 8 px under the field, as wide as the field, its rows 8 px round, the pointed one grey, the picked one medium with a tick.

**Spacing**: labels #808080, 12 px over their control; #999 is for placeholders and the text of anything off (buttons, segments, links; Figma 5077:7); 16 px lines (Figma 5008:10789; checkbox rows too, and row words). The form's groups stand 20 px apart on every screen, so from one control to the next is 48 with a label's 16 and 12 to its field (`src/index.css`, on the sidebar and the sheet; the header, `data-head`, keeps 16). Controls are 40 px tall on wide screens, 48 in the phone's sheet. Keep the 48 when changing any of these. No hint texts about how the controls work next to them.

**Download** (`DownloadBar`, 40 px under the form, sticking to the sidebar's foot): one black button. While something's in the way it says the next step in the infinitive, as a button's verb («Выбрать дилера», «Добавить наименование», «Указать цену»; the form's first empty field in its order, `unfilled`), or a wrong value as it is («Цена слишком низкая», «Текст шире 501 мм»), with several items the next one in work's first fault, then a grey «1/5», what's left to do before the download — every empty and wrong field over the whole list and the dealership, none while one is left (`count`, `stepsLeft`); a click leads there (focusing the field, in the tap itself so a phone's keyboard comes up; on phones opening the sheet first). Once nothing is in the way, «Скачать PDF» / «Скачать ZIP», the format only, never how many. ⌘S (Ctrl+S) presses it on every generator, whatever it says, instead of the browser saving the page; its tooltip says so («Скачать PDF ⌘S») while it's the download. Not a greyed-out button with a warning: the bright button is pressed even unread and teaches the step.

**«Поделиться»** (`LinkButtons`, passed to the bar as `links`), on every generator: over the download on wide screens, 8 px off it, as wide; beside it on phones, half each; shown only once the download is on. It sends the link to the file, the page address with `pdf=1` (see «Links with settings»): it mostly goes to a dealer, who wants the file, not the editor. With Alt (Option) held it sends the address as it is, the editor with these settings, for the team; while Alt is down the button says «Ссылка на редактор». Where the browser can send a file (`navigator.canShare` with files: phones, Safari, Chrome; not Firefox) the price card, plate frame and hours sign send the PDF itself through the share sheet instead (`LinkButtons`' `file`), made ahead 400 ms after the settings last changed, as the sheet opens only straight from the click; while it's being made, and for the livery's ZIP (not a type browsers share; megabytes), the link goes as below. Else on phones (`pointer: coarse` with `navigator.share`) the link through the system's share sheet; elsewhere copied, the button saying «Ссылка на файл скопирована» (or «Ссылка на редактор скопирована») in Regular, #808080, for two seconds, still meanwhile (no hover, no click). The name tag, business card and price tag send the file only, never a link (their list in the address made a link of kilobytes; `fileOnly`): the button is there only where `canShareFiles`, the name tag sending the ZIP's three files (PDF, photo, spec; `tagsFiles`) as the sheet takes no ZIP, else its PDF alone; Alt does nothing there. Pressed before the file is ready, it's made then: Chrome sends it, Safari may refuse.

**Resets** are on the canvas, by what they reset, never in the sidebar beside «Поделиться» (one slip lost a whole set-up with no undo): the cards' in their action column, the livery's sheets and the plate frame's strip at the end of their caption (`CaptionReset` in `staff.tsx`, the cards' icon, #808080, in view while there's something to reset; its tooltip says what goes: «Вернуть борта по умолчанию», «Вернуть стекло по умолчанию», «Вернуть рамку по умолчанию»). A reset brings back what the address keeps.

## Item lists (`src/ui/staff.tsx`)

The name tag, business card, price tag and price card hold several items (people, goods, cards) through `useStaff`. One is selected and edited in the sidebar. Every item is edged at 1 px 10% black, at 40% on hover (`ITEM_EDGE`); in the manual mode the selected one is clear and the others dimmed to 40%, clear on hover. The click that picks an item works across the canvas's whole row (the items stand in one column); a click above, under or between them leaves none selected (except on the price card, where one is always selected).

An item's number and actions stand in one column right of it, 8 px off it from its top (`ItemFrame`, Figma 5030:11397, 5008:10960): the number 30 px wide with 5 px under it, from 1 once there are two; the actions under it while the pointer is in its row (on touch screens under the selected one only), acting on it without selecting it: «Дублировать» (a copy right after it, selected, its first field focused), «Сбросить» (while there's something to reset) and «Удалить» (a bin, as on phones; while there's more than one; selects the next), icon buttons with a 1 px stroke, #808080, black on hover with no tile, in that order, one not offered leaving no gap. Keys (`useKeys`, ⌘ on a Mac, Ctrl elsewhere, `src/ui/keys.ts`, matched by the key's place so the Russian layout works): ⌘D duplicates the selected item and ⌘↩ (Ctrl+Enter) presses «Добавить», anywhere, a field too (⌘N is the browser's own); out of a field Delete or Backspace removes it, ↑ ↓ select the one before or after it, ⌥↑ ⌥↓ move it up or down the list (the order things print in), Enter goes into its first field, ⌘C copies it (as a header row of the page's `columns` and its row, so a spreadsheet or another generator takes it; ⌘V on the same page puts the copy back whole after the selected one, rows from a spreadsheet too), ⌘Z brings back the one just removed (in a field it undoes the typing); Esc leaves a field, unless a list open in it took Esc. In a field ↑ ↓ step a number: the price card's prices by 5 000 ₽ (`TextInput`'s `step`, the first step landing on a round 5 000, an empty field taking its placeholder), the hours sign's times by half an hour. The keys are said only in tooltips («Дублировать ⌘D», «Добавить ⌘↩», «Вернуть»: «⌘Z»), never beside the controls. An item's faults stand under it, centred, as the canvas's captions (not over it: the item edited would jump as they come and go), 13 px #E30; a click on them goes to the field at fault, selecting the item, as the download's note does (`ItemAlert`). A removed item leaves «Удаляем · Вернуть» in its place, 40 px tall, for four seconds, led by the bin in a 1 px ring that runs out over the four seconds (none with reduced motion; as Telegram's undo; not «Удалено · Вернуть ×», the cross at the end read as a hint's close), the bin closing it at once (`Removed`).

«Добавить» (`AddTile`), a secondary button as wide as its text, centred under the list, adds an item, selects it, focuses its first field and scrolls it to the middle of the canvas with the button under it in view . Any item selected (clicked, duplicated, pasted, brought back, picked or moved with the arrows, gone to from the download's note) comes to the middle of the canvas on wide screens; the last ones had no room under them, so the canvas grows at its foot by what's missing while one of them is centred (`centreOn`). An item added empty is fresh: its empty fields aren't errors until it's left; empty fields on any other item show as errors at once.

Modes under «Данные» (name tag, business card, price tag): «Вручную» and «Из таблицы». In «Из таблицы» the template (`TableSource`), then the loaded file's name to replace it, stands right under the switch, 8 px off it; until a file is loaded the whole preview is the upload. The preview takes a dropped table in either mode, with nothing on the page saying so, and loading switches to «Из таблицы», replacing the list. There's one list, not one per mode: clicking an item in «Из таблицы» switches to «Вручную» with it selected, and the first edit makes the list a manual one. Rows copied from a spreadsheet and pasted into any field fill in a row each (replacing the item pasted into if empty). `src/nametag/table.ts` reads the first sheet itself (fflate + DOMParser: shared and inline strings), finds a header row by its titles in any column order, and keeps line breaks in a cell (Alt+Enter). A single whole-name column («ФИО», «Ф.И.О.», «Сотрудник», «Фамилия Имя Отчество», `FULL_NAME`) fills in the name and surname where the row has none (`fromFullName`). CSV isn't taken: Russian Excel on Windows saves it in cp1251.

Name and surname stay two fields, but a whole name pasted into «Имя», or left in it while «Фамилия» is empty, goes into both (`fullNameField`). `splitFullName` drops a patronymic (-вич / -вна, or the father's name with оглы / кызы) and reads the order from where it stood: last, «Петров Иван Сергеевич»; in the middle, «Иван Сергеевич Петров»; without one, two words are «Имя Фамилия» in the field and «Фамилия Имя» in a ФИО column; more words are left alone, hyphenated ones are one word, and a two-word name stays whole once the surname is filled in first.

## Links with settings

The price card, livery, plate frame and hours sign keep their settings in the page address, so a set-up result is sent as a link («Поделиться», or the address bar). The page reads them on load and writes them back with `history.replaceState`; only what differs from the defaults goes in, and anything unknown or implausible falls back to the default. Messengers may encode a pasted link again (Telegram turns `%0A` into `%250A`), so `linkParams` decodes escapes left after decoding, up to twice. Keep the names stable, as links get sent around; adding a setting means reading it in the page's initial state and writing it in its `useLinkState` call.

- Price card: `model` (UMO 5 is the default and comes first in the switch, Figma 5015:11121; `umo8` picks UMO 8), `trim`, `full` (seven digits, else the trim's price), `credit` (on: `auto` follows the full price, seven digits are set by hand), `link` (left out while it's the model's page), `pdf` (saves the cards' PDF to the downloads under its own name, `UMO-8-ULTRA.pdf` or `UMO_price-cards.pdf`, as «Скачать» does, then opens it in the tab, no editor — the tab's own Ctrl+S would name it by the blob's id; the same name is the PDF's title, which Chrome shows on the tab and over the PDF; a card at fault opens the editor; sent as `download_price_card` with `via: link`); these are the first card's, the next cards' carry their number (`model2`, `full3`, `link2`) and `cards` says how many (up to 20; left out for one).
- Livery: `model` (umo8), `link`, `top`, `bottom` (line breaks as `%0A`; `bottom` left out while it's the model's default), `off` (any of `qr,top,bottom,rdealer,rslogan`; the last two leave the dealer or slogan off the rear window; `off=rear` is ignored), `rear=on`, `rbottom` (the rear window's slogan once it differs from the sides'; `rtop` is no longer read), `show` (`dims,seams`), `size=large` (the 60 mm bottom text), `qr=small`, `pdf` (downloads the ZIP once the font is in, the editor staying with the car, as the name tag's; sheets at fault only show what's wrong).
- Plate frame: `name` (a leading «UMO» is dropped, as the prefix is fixed), `text` (the whole line, which also turns the free mode on), `align=center`, `pdf` (as the price card's: `UMO_plate-frame_501x21.pdf`, once the font is in; a line at fault opens the editor).
- Hours sign: `from`, `to` (a listed dealer's umo.auto hours when left out), `name` (a leading «UMO» is dropped), `address` (once it isn't the dealer's), `phone` (once it isn't the dealer's; put through the field's mask, so bare digits do), `site` (once edited), `pdf` (as the price card's: `UMO_hours-sign.pdf`; a sign at fault opens the editor).

- Price tag: read on load only, never written back (a list would make the address huge): `dealer`, then `n1`, `c1`, `p1`, `s1` (name, code, price, caption: `set` and `pack` for «за комплект», «за упаковку», else as written), `n2`… while there's a name; `pdf` as the price card's, `UMO_price-tags.pdf`.

A `pdf` link saves and opens the file through `saveAndOpenPdf` in `src/ui/share.ts`; each PDF's title is its file name.

## Files dropped back

The price tag, business card, name tag and hours sign generators open their own downloads again: the PDF carries what it was made from as JSON in its document info (`writePdfData` in `src/ui/pdfDataWrite.ts`, under `UMOPriceTags`, `UMOBusinessCards` — dealership, QR link, «Обложка у всех», «Метки реза», the staff — `UMONameTags` and `UMOHoursSign`), saved without object streams so `readPdfData` (`src/ui/pdfData.ts`) finds it with no PDF parser; dropped on the page or picked in the upload («Загрузить таблицу или PDF», the name tag's «Загрузить таблицу или ZIP»), it replaces the list as a table does. Any other PDF is said to have no cards. The price card, livery and plate frame don't: their settings are in the link.

## Names

The name tag and business card take only letters in a name and surname, with spaces, hyphens and dashes, apostrophes (Д'Артаньян) and dots (initials) (`nameFault` in `src/ui/names.ts`): a digit is «Имя: цифры», another sign «Фамилия: лишние знаки «@»», an error red on the item and blocking the download, never taken out quietly (it's most often a table pasted askew).

## Obscene words

The plate frame, name tag, business card and livery refuse obscene words (`src/ui/profanity.ts`, `hasProfanity`): the check adds an issue in `buildStrip`, `buildTag` (per field: «Имя: недопустимое слово») and `setText` (on the lines holding the word; all of a text's lines when the word is spaced out across them), turning the text red and blocking the download. The word itself is never shown, nothing is starred out. It catches the Russian мат roots (хуй, пизд, еб at the start of a word or after a prefix, блядь/бля, муд-, пидор, залуп, манда, гондон, шлюха, сука), vulgar words out of place on a dealer's frame or badge (грёбаный, жопа, говно, дерьмо, срать, ссать, хер, дрочить, пердеть, трахать, сиськи, шалава, мразь, ублюдок, чмо) and a few English ones (fuck, cunt, shit, bitch…), through disguises: case, ё, Latin and digit look-alikes (a, c, e, o, p, x, y, k, m, h, t, b→в; 0, 1, 3, 4, 6, @), letters spaced, dotted or hyphenated apart, a star for a letter. Roots that occur inside ordinary words are anchored so хлеб, небо, себе, вебинар, рубля, гребля, гребень, мандарин, команда, мудрый, страхуем, гондола, сравнение, сруб, ссылка, ссуда, SsangYong, Херсон, херес, страх, трахея and Scunthorpe pass. Jokes and plain insults («Шляпа полная», дурак, лох, задница) are deliberately not caught. Client-side only, no library: extend the lists and check ordinary words against them.

## Metrika goals

`MetrikaHits` in `src/main.tsx` sends a view per page path (not per query or hash change: the generators rewrite the query with every setting). The generators reach Metrika goals (`goal` in `src/ui/metrika.ts`, JavaScript events set up in Metrika by the same ids): `download_<generator>` on «Скачать» with what was made (model, trim, parts on, number of tags or cards; never what was typed, except texts not from the suggestions: the plate frame's `line` when it isn't a listed dealer, the livery's `dealerText`, `taglineText`, `rearTaglineText` when they aren't a listed dealer or tagline, and the QR `link` when it isn't the model's page (price card, livery) or the site made up from the dealer's name (business card)), `copy_link` on «Поделиться» (`pdf`: whether it was the file's link), `open_menu` when the generators menu is opened, `open_help` when «Инструкция» is opened (`page`), `dealer_link` when a `pdf` link names the dealer (see «Dealers»).

## Dealers

`src/data/dealers.ts` holds every dealer (`DEALERS`): umo.auto's (`dealers.json`, parsed by `scripts/fetch-dealers.mjs`) merged with the hand-kept `dealers-own.json`, where an entry with a new name adds a dealer signed but not on umo.auto yet (name, city, short address as umo.auto writes them), and one with an existing name only adds to it: `aka`, the names it's also called by («Мажор» for Major), and `phone`, the UMO showroom's own line (umo.auto has no phones; taken from the showroom's card on Yandex Maps, the one titled UMO at umo.auto's coordinates, as dealers often give UMO a line of its own beside their other brands'; `phoneOf`). Edit that file by hand; never put the internal dealer sheet's candidates or contracts there. Their names are the dealer fields' suggestions (`DEALER_NAMES`); `dealerOf` finds one by its name, with «UMO» or without, for its address and hours. `findDealers` finds dealers by what people write: words of the name, city (with «питер», «спб», «мск», «екб», «нн», «ростов»), `aka` and address without the region; Latin also read as Russian letters (Major → майор, VERRA → верра); a word's other case («во владимире»), its start or a one-letter typo count; words no dealer has («салон») don't. The name itself wins; else the dealers with the most words matched. The dealer fields' suggestions narrow by it (`dealerFilter`, ComboField's `filter`), so «питер» or «алтуфьево» shows its dealers.

A `pdf` link may name the dealer loosely, as said (`dealer=агат%20во%20владимире`), and the page finds it (`LinkDealer` in `src/ui/LinkDealer.tsx`, around the price tag, business card, hours sign, plate frame and livery routes in `src/main.tsx`, on `dealer`, `name` or the livery's `top`, which takes the name with «UMO»): before the generator reads the link, `findDealers` turns the words into the dealer's name when one is found and every word is some dealer's (`knowsEveryWord`); one found with a word no dealer has («Рольф Сити»: Рольф Волгоградский), a screen «Это ваш салон?» shows it, several, «Какой у вас салон?» lists them, with addresses, plus «Моего салона нет в списке»; that, or none found, opens the editor instead of the file with the words in the dealer's field, to be written as on the sign. The Metrika goal `dealer_link` says how it went: `result` `found`, `picked` (with `choices`) or `none` (with `said`, the words, to add dealers and names missing). A link without `pdf` is left alone, as its name may be the dealer's own line. Names go into the link in Cyrillic with spaces as `%20`.
