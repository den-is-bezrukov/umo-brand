# Generators: what they share

## Generator form (`src/ui/form.tsx`)

The sidebar is 320 px plus a 1 px stroke on wide screens (Figma 4844:6865 price card, 4900:4588 livery).

**Header** (`GeneratorHeader`, Figma 5098:5487, the same on every screen): the menu icon and the breadcrumbs «Бренд UMO» → `/` · «Носители» → `/#materials` in Medium on 16 px lines, 12 px after the icon; on phones only, the size icon across from them (see «Generators on phones» below). Under them the generators' line (`GeneratorRing`): scrolled natively by a finger, trackpad or wheel, and picked as a drum — the name the line comes to rest on, 24 px from the edge, black as it goes, opens the instant the finger is off and the line still (`scrollend`; 150 ms with no scrolling where a browser lacks it), a tap opening one too. It goes round: the generators (`GENERATORS`, alphabetical: Бейдж, Визитка, Ливрея, Прайс-карта, Рамка номера, Режимник, Ценник) three times over, the middle copy's current one at the edge, so there's a whole turn to scroll either way; each page puts its own there. 24 px Medium, others #BFBFBF, #808080 on hover, 20 px apart. The menu icon turns into the guide's «Свернуть» (icons from `src/icons/toc.ts`) and the line, the rest of the form and the download give way to every generator, 20 px under the crumbs, 24 px Medium 20 px apart, the current one black (Figma 5099:5825; `html[data-menu]` in `src/index.css`): the same header, not a second sheet over the form.

**Controls**: segments, fields, the QR link field, a combobox (`ComboField`: a text field with suggestions in a popover under it, narrowed as you type), checkboxes (2 px tick, 8 px to the label, 4 px round), the size switch («Крупнее» / «Мельче» with arrows out or in). Styled per Figma 5015:11121: 8 px corners on segments, fields, buttons and the suggestions list; segments, tabs and sections have no hover, the picked segment a white tile edged at 10% on the grey and the others' text #808080; a focused field edged at 40%; a field with an error is never edged in red, its label turns red instead (`ALERT_LABEL`: the label of a `Field`, an `OptionalField`'s checkbox or the business card's «Дилер» finds the control's aria-invalid under it with `group-has`); icons a 2 px stroke with square ends and bevelled corners; row words (the size switch, «Редактировать») and quiet lines #808080, black on hover; secondary buttons («Поделиться») edged at 10%, at 40% on hover; the suggestions list white, 2 px inside its edge, with a soft shadow, 8 px under the field, as wide as the field, its rows 8 px round, the pointed one grey, the picked one medium with a tick.

**Spacing**: labels #808080, 12 px over their control; #999 is for placeholders and the text of anything off (buttons, segments, links; Figma 5077:7); 16 px lines (Figma 5008:10789; checkbox rows too, and row words). The form's groups stand 20 px apart on every screen, so from one control to the next is 48 with a label's 16 and 12 to its field (`src/index.css`, on the sidebar and the sheet; the header, `data-head`, keeps 16). Controls are 40 px tall on wide screens, 48 in the phone's sheet. Keep the 48 when changing any of these. No hint texts about how the controls work next to them.

**Download** (`DownloadBar`, 40 px under the form, sticking to the sidebar's foot): one black button. While something's in the way it says the next step in the infinitive, as a button's verb («Выбрать дилера», «Добавить наименование», «Указать цену»; the form's first empty field in its order, `unfilled`), or a wrong value as it is («Цена слишком низкая», «Текст шире 501 мм»), with several items the next one in work's first fault, then a grey «1/5», what's left to do before the download — every empty and wrong field over the whole list and the dealership, none while one is left (`count`, `stepsLeft`); a click leads there (focusing the field, in the tap itself so a phone's keyboard comes up; on phones opening the sheet first). Once nothing is in the way, «Скачать PDF» / «Скачать ZIP», the format only, never how many. Not a greyed-out button with a warning: the bright button is pressed even unread and teaches the step.

**«Поделиться»** (`LinkButtons`, passed to the bar as `links`), on the pages that keep their settings in the link: over the download on wide screens, 8 px off it, as wide; beside it on phones, half each; shown only once the download is on. On phones (`pointer: coarse` with `navigator.share`) through the system's share sheet; elsewhere the page address is copied and the button says «Ссылка с настройками скопирована» in Regular, #808080, for two seconds, still meanwhile (no hover, no click).

**Resets** are on the canvas, by what they reset, never in the sidebar beside «Поделиться» (one slip lost a whole set-up with no undo): the cards' in their action column, the livery's sheets and the plate frame's strip at the end of their caption (`CaptionReset` in `staff.tsx`, the cards' icon, #808080, in view while there's something to reset; its tooltip says what goes: «Вернуть борта по умолчанию», «Вернуть стекло по умолчанию», «Вернуть рамку по умолчанию»). A reset brings back what the address keeps.

## Generators on phones (`src/ui/mobile.tsx`)

Figma 5098:5806 «Mobile Layout», on every generator below `md`. The canvas, 10 px in, over a sheet 60% of the screen (Figma 5098:5487: shadowed 0 10 30 at 10% black, 24 px in and 24 px round on top) holding the sidebar, the download over its foot on a white gradient, 24 px in all round plus the screen's bottom safe area under it (`viewport-fit=cover` in `index.html`, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`; touching the edge, the black button turned Safari 26's bar black) (5098:5539; `InFoot` in `src/ui/phone.ts`, read by `DownloadBar`). The download and «Поделиться» step aside while a text field has the focus (the keyboard is up). The sheet changes height in 500 ms on cubic-bezier(0.67, 0, 0.33, 1). The size icon in the header (`SheetSize`, chevrons out) opens the form over the whole screen (only the list's row left over it), «Свернуть» putting it back; there's no grabber or swipe.

A list's pictures (and the livery's sheets) are swiped one at a time under a row (5105:7604): delete, a bin, on the left, copy on the right, between them «‹ n/Name ↺ ›» (`number` and `label` on a slide: «2/Иван Петров», «1/UMO 5 PRO», «0/Обложка», «1/Левый борт»; the reset right after the name while there's something to reset; «‹ ›» unless `arrows=0`); one picture has a row over it with its name in the middle («Табличка», «Поле печати», an item's name), the reset after it, copy on the right, and no caption under the picture. Pictures are as large as the strip allows with what stands under them (an item's faults, a caption; measured, `Fitted`), 20 px off its sides, 10 over and under. A tap on a picture folds the sheet away only when that makes it at least 15% larger (`GROWS`: the price card, the sign, the rear window, never a landscape item); then a shrink icon under it brings the sheet back (5105:8488). Icons on the canvas are Figma's outlines as exported, a 1 px stroke; those of the header and form 2 px (menu and close black, the rest #808080).

Every control in the sheet is 48 px tall (fields, segments, comboboxes, the download and «Поделиться»), through `in-[[data-sheet]]:` on the controls, and a checkbox row takes 48 px with its look unchanged (16 px padding given back by margins). A combobox in the sheet opens as a screen of its own, as a search (`InSheet`): its field with «Готово» at the top, the suggestions in rows across the rest, with no keyboard until its field is tapped. A copy made on a phone isn't focused and is fresh, its empty fields no errors until it's left. Each page passes its `sidebar`, `download` and `slides` (`InSlide` strips `ItemFrame` and `Removed` there). No «Добавить» on phones yet.

## Item lists (`src/ui/staff.tsx`)

The name tag, business card, price tag and price card hold several items (people, goods, cards) through `useStaff`. One is selected and edited in the sidebar. Every item is edged at 1 px 10% black, at 40% on hover (`ITEM_EDGE`); in the manual mode the selected one is clear and the others dimmed to 40%, clear on hover. The click that picks an item works across the canvas's whole row (the items stand in one column); a click above, under or between them leaves none selected (except on the price card, where one is always selected).

An item's number and actions stand in one column right of it, 8 px off it from its top (`ItemFrame`, Figma 5030:11397, 5008:10960): the number 30 px wide with 5 px under it, from 1 once there are two; the actions under it while the pointer is in its row (on touch screens under the selected one only), acting on it without selecting it: «Дублировать» (a copy right after it, selected, its first field focused), «Сбросить» (while there's something to reset) and «Удалить» (while there's more than one; selects the next; so does Delete or Backspace while the focus isn't in a field), icon buttons with a 1 px stroke, #808080, black on hover with no tile, in that order, one not offered leaving no gap. A removed item leaves «Удалено · Вернуть ×» in its place, 40 px tall, for four seconds, the cross closing it at once, a 1 px ring round it running out over the four seconds (none with reduced motion) (`Removed`).

«Добавить» (`AddTile`), a secondary button as wide as its text, centred under the list, adds an item, selects it, focuses its first field and scrolls it to the middle of the canvas with the button under it in view (an item selected by a click isn't scrolled). An item added empty is fresh: its empty fields aren't errors until it's left; empty fields on any other item show as errors at once.

Modes under «Данные» (name tag, business card, price tag): «Вручную» and «Из таблицы». In «Из таблицы» the template (`TableSource`), then the loaded file's name to replace it, stands right under the switch, 8 px off it; until a file is loaded the whole preview is the upload. The preview takes a dropped table in either mode, with nothing on the page saying so, and loading switches to «Из таблицы», replacing the list. There's one list, not one per mode: clicking an item in «Из таблицы» switches to «Вручную» with it selected, and the first edit makes the list a manual one. Rows copied from a spreadsheet and pasted into any field fill in a row each (replacing the item pasted into if empty). `src/nametag/table.ts` reads the first sheet itself (fflate + DOMParser: shared and inline strings), finds a header row by its titles in any column order, and keeps line breaks in a cell (Alt+Enter). A single whole-name column («ФИО», «Ф.И.О.», «Сотрудник», «Фамилия Имя Отчество», `FULL_NAME`) fills in the name and surname where the row has none (`fromFullName`). CSV isn't taken: Russian Excel on Windows saves it in cp1251.

Name and surname stay two fields, but a whole name pasted into «Имя», or left in it while «Фамилия» is empty, goes into both (`fullNameField`). `splitFullName` drops a patronymic (-вич / -вна, or the father's name with оглы / кызы) and reads the order from where it stood: last, «Петров Иван Сергеевич»; in the middle, «Иван Сергеевич Петров»; without one, two words are «Имя Фамилия» in the field and «Фамилия Имя» in a ФИО column; more words are left alone, hyphenated ones are one word, and a two-word name stays whole once the surname is filled in first.

## Links with settings

The price card, livery, plate frame and hours sign keep their settings in the page address, so a set-up result is sent as a link («Поделиться», or the address bar). The page reads them on load and writes them back with `history.replaceState`; only what differs from the defaults goes in, and anything unknown or implausible falls back to the default. Messengers may encode a pasted link again (Telegram turns `%0A` into `%250A`), so `linkParams` decodes escapes left after decoding, up to twice. Keep the names stable, as links get sent around; adding a setting means reading it in the page's initial state and writing it in its `useLinkState` call.

- Price card: `model` (UMO 5 is the default and comes first in the switch, Figma 5015:11121; `umo8` picks UMO 8), `trim`, `full` (seven digits, else the trim's price), `credit` (on: `auto` follows the full price, seven digits are set by hand), `link` (left out while it's the model's page), `pdf` (saves the cards' PDF to the downloads under its own name, `UMO-8-ULTRA.pdf` or `UMO_price-cards.pdf`, as «Скачать» does, then opens it in the tab, no editor — the tab's own Ctrl+S would name it by the blob's id; the same name is the PDF's title, which Chrome shows on the tab and over the PDF; a card at fault opens the editor; sent as `download_price_card` with `via: link`); these are the first card's, the next cards' carry their number (`model2`, `full3`, `link2`) and `cards` says how many (up to 20; left out for one).
- Livery: `model` (umo8), `link`, `top`, `bottom` (line breaks as `%0A`; `bottom` left out while it's the model's default), `off` (any of `qr,top,bottom,rdealer,rslogan`; the last two leave the dealer or slogan off the rear window; `off=rear` is ignored), `rear=on`, `rbottom` (the rear window's slogan once it differs from the sides'; `rtop` is no longer read), `show` (`dims,seams`), `size=large` (the 60 mm bottom text), `qr=small`.
- Plate frame: `name` (a leading «UMO» is dropped, as the prefix is fixed), `text` (the whole line, which also turns the free mode on), `align=center`, `pdf` (as the price card's: `UMO_plate-frame_501x21.pdf`, once the font is in; a line at fault opens the editor).
- Hours sign: `from`, `to` (a listed dealer's umo.auto hours when left out), `name` (a leading «UMO» is dropped), `address` (once it isn't the dealer's), `phone` (put through the field's mask, so bare digits do), `site` (once edited), `pdf` (as the price card's: `UMO_hours-sign.pdf`; a sign at fault opens the editor).

- Price tag: read on load only, never written back (a list would make the address huge): `dealer`, then `n1`, `c1`, `p1`, `s1` (name, code, price, caption: `set` and `pack` for «за комплект», «за упаковку», else as written), `n2`… while there's a name; `pdf` as the price card's, `UMO_price-tags.pdf`.

A `pdf` link saves and opens the file through `saveAndOpenPdf` in `src/ui/share.ts`; each PDF's title is its file name.

## Files dropped back

The price tag, business card, name tag and hours sign generators open their own downloads again: the PDF carries what it was made from as JSON in its document info (`writePdfData` in `src/ui/pdfDataWrite.ts`, under `UMOPriceTags`, `UMOBusinessCards` — dealership, QR link, «Обложка у всех», «Метки реза», the staff — `UMONameTags` and `UMOHoursSign`), saved without object streams so `readPdfData` (`src/ui/pdfData.ts`) finds it with no PDF parser; dropped on the page or picked in the upload («Загрузить таблицу или PDF», the name tag's «Загрузить таблицу или ZIP»), it replaces the list as a table does. Any other PDF is said to have no cards. The price card, livery and plate frame don't: their settings are in the link.

## Names

The name tag and business card take only letters in a name and surname, with spaces, hyphens and dashes, apostrophes (Д'Артаньян) and dots (initials) (`nameFault` in `src/ui/names.ts`): a digit is «Имя: цифры», another sign «Фамилия: лишние знаки «@»», an error red on the item and blocking the download, never taken out quietly (it's most often a table pasted askew).

## Obscene words

The plate frame, name tag, business card and livery refuse obscene words (`src/ui/profanity.ts`, `hasProfanity`): the check adds an issue in `buildStrip`, `buildTag` (per field: «Имя: недопустимое слово») and `setText` (on the lines holding the word; all of a text's lines when the word is spaced out across them), turning the text red and blocking the download. The word itself is never shown, nothing is starred out. It catches the Russian мат roots (хуй, пизд, еб at the start of a word or after a prefix, блядь/бля, муд-, пидор, залуп, манда, гондон, шлюха, сука), vulgar words out of place on a dealer's frame or badge (грёбаный, жопа, говно, дерьмо, срать, ссать, хер, дрочить, пердеть, трахать, сиськи, шалава, мразь, ублюдок, чмо) and a few English ones (fuck, cunt, shit, bitch…), through disguises: case, ё, Latin and digit look-alikes (a, c, e, o, p, x, y, k, m, h, t, b→в; 0, 1, 3, 4, 6, @), letters spaced, dotted or hyphenated apart, a star for a letter. Roots that occur inside ordinary words are anchored so хлеб, небо, себе, вебинар, рубля, гребля, гребень, мандарин, команда, мудрый, страхуем, гондола, сравнение, сруб, ссылка, ссуда, SsangYong, Херсон, херес, страх, трахея and Scunthorpe pass. Jokes and plain insults («Шляпа полная», дурак, лох, задница) are deliberately not caught. Client-side only, no library: extend the lists and check ordinary words against them.

## Metrika goals

`MetrikaHits` in `src/main.tsx` sends a view per page path (not per query or hash change: the generators rewrite the query with every setting). The generators reach Metrika goals (`goal` in `src/ui/metrika.ts`, JavaScript events set up in Metrika by the same ids): `download_<generator>` on «Скачать» with what was made (model, trim, parts on, number of tags or cards; never what was typed, except texts not from the suggestions: the plate frame's `line` when it isn't a listed dealer, the livery's `dealerText`, `taglineText`, `rearTaglineText` when they aren't a listed dealer or tagline, and the QR `link` when it isn't the model's page (price card, livery) or the site made up from the dealer's name (business card)), `copy_link` on «Поделиться», `open_menu` when the generators menu is opened.

## Chat agents

A dealer may skip the generators and ask a chat instead. `public/llms.txt` is the instruction for it: ask what's needed, make the price card, price tags, hours sign and plate frame as a link with settings and `pdf=1` (the page then opens the PDF itself, no editor), send to the generator by link for everything else (business cards, name tags, livery); a dealer with a goods table in Excel is sent to the price tag editor and its template. The dealer is looked up and confirmed, not asked for: `umo.autos/dealers.txt` (name, address, hours from `src/data/dealers.json`, built by the `ai-page` plugin, at build and in dev) lists them; ChatGPT and Claude read it by the link, Alice gets it after the instruction in her field (`#dealers` in the page), so the hours sign asks only the hours (when they differ) and the phone, and its link carries just the name and phone. Names and addresses go into the link in Cyrillic with spaces as `%20`; Alice makes such a link clickable. It's served as is at `/llms.txt` and inside `umo.autos/ai`, a plain page with no React, 288 px wide at most (`src/ai/page.html`, filled with the instruction by the `ai-page` plugin in `vite.config.ts`, at build and in dev, where it reloads itself as either file changes), so an agent opening either reads the text. robots.txt allows both, as ChatGPT and Claude honour it when opening a link.

`/ai`, «Материалы для дилеров» (24/28 px, −0.01 em): the logo on top as the guide's header (120×24, 24 px in) but centred, the form centred on the screen under it. Two ways, 32 px under the title, with no group labels: «Сделать с @ Алисой AI» (filled #7A55FF, her colour on alice.yandex.ru, her mark from Alice Okniks inside the phrase, 4 px either side) and «Собрать в редакторе», black as the generators' «Скачать», to the price card generator. Under them, 8 px apart as they are, «Другой способ», a secondary button with a chevron (white, a 10% black border, as «Скопировать инструкцию»), folds ChatGPT and Claude, secondary too (Regular, as the copy), with their marks from Simple Icons in black and no brand colours, «Скопировать инструкцию» and, folded once more, «Инструкция для ИИ» with the instruction itself, left-aligned. Its link shows the site's social image (`public/social-image.png`, Figma 4876:3660) with «Материалы для дилеров UMO», set in the page's own head, as the `site-meta` plugin doesn't reach it. The page's head links `/llms.txt` as `rel=alternate` text, for an agent given the page: a closed fold's text is in the HTML but not in the visible text. ChatGPT (`chatgpt.com/?prompt=`) and Claude (`claude.ai/new?q=`) open with a line asking to read umo.autos/llms.txt; Alice reads no links, so she opens with the whole instruction in her field (`alice.yandex.ru/?set_text=`; 8,000 Cyrillic characters reach her field and send, without signing in), in «Эксперт» (`model=alice-agents-expert`): in «Авто» a free-form answer («пятёрку про и восьмёрку ультра») goes to her web search, which forgets the instruction and quotes prices from the news. The instruction is in English for the agent, with what it says to the dealer as Russian phrases and a line insisting on Russian; its final message tells how to print on Windows (Ctrl + P, A3, or the copy in «Загрузки» to a copy centre); for the hours sign and plate frame, that the file goes from «Загрузки» to a sign maker or the frame maker, with what to order. The instruction and the dealers, about 14,800 characters, reach Alice's field and send (checked 2026-10-12). Each sends the Metrika goal `ai_open` with `chat`. The instruction repeats the price card's trims, `DEFAULTS` prices and link parameters, and the hours sign's and plate frame's parameters and file names: change it with them.
