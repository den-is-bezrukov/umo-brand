# Plate frame (`/plate-frame`)

The dealer's line on a number plate frame (Figma: UMO | Evrone, node 4970:2419; guide section 4970:2280, anchor `plate-frame`): one line in the 501×21 mm print field of a standard 522×132 frame, the dealer's marketing name («UMO АГАТ Владимир»): «UMO » is fixed (`PREFIX`), and only the name is typed or picked (the «Дилер» combobox, offering the dealers without «UMO », empty at first, its placeholder «Центр» standing grey on the frame after the white «UMO », never in the PDF). A link with `text`, even empty (`/plate-frame?text`), opens a hidden mode with the whole line free («UMO Центр» grey as its placeholder), linked from nowhere. CoFo Sans Medium 18 mm, −1% tracking, the case-sensitive forms on (`case`; opentype.js doesn't apply the feature, so `setLine` substitutes the glyphs itself), the capitals' top 2.5 mm under the field's top (`BASELINE` in `src/plate/frame.ts`, from the 680-unit cap height; Figma has 3, raised by half a millimetre for optical balance), set from the left edge or centred. Text wider than the field or with a character the font lacks turns red and blocks the download. No editing on the frame itself: a double click edited the line there until 2026-10-13, dropped as hidden and skipping the dealers' suggestions. `UMO_plate-frame_501x21.pdf` is the field at 1:1, no bleed, the text in outlines filled with a spot colour named White (white ink for UV printing on the black frame), over a black stand-in for the frame on a layer of its own, «Фон — не печатать», set not to print (an optional content group with PrintState OFF). The plate and frame are the Figma vectors in `PlateArt.tsx` (1 px = 1 mm, frame at 195,86 of the 912×304 picture); the guide draws the strip with live `<text>`, the generator with the outlines that go into the PDF.

The PDF's title is its file name. A link with `pdf` saves `UMO_plate-frame_501x21.pdf` to the downloads and opens it with no editor; a line at fault opens the editor (see «Links with settings» in `docs/generators.md`).

«Скачать шаблон», centred under the frame, `UMO_plate-frame_template.pdf`: the same page, the text live in CoFo Sans Medium in the same White spot colour over the same unprinted black (`stripTemplate`); `umo.autos/plate-frame?template` gives «UMO Центр» on the left (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
