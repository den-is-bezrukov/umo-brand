# Plate frame (`/plate-frame`)

The point's line on a number plate frame (guide anchor `plate-frame`): one line in the 501×21 mm print field of a standard 522×132 frame, the point's marketing name («<Бренд> Точка Город»): «<Бренд> » is fixed (`PREFIX`), and only the name is typed or picked (the point combobox, offering the points without «<Бренд> », empty at first, its placeholder «Центр» standing grey on the frame after the white «<Бренд> », never in the PDF). A link with `text`, even empty (`/plate-frame?text`), opens a hidden mode with the whole line free («<Бренд> Центр» grey as its placeholder), linked from nowhere; a brand without points has only that mode, its site's address the placeholder. the brand font Medium at the size and tracking the brand sets (`PLATE_FRAME` in its `plate-frame.ts`, numbers in its `README.md`), the case-sensitive forms on (`case`; opentype.js doesn't apply the feature, so `setLine` substitutes the glyphs itself), the capitals' top `top` mm under the field's top (`BASELINE` in `src/plate/frame.ts`, from the font's cap height), set from the left edge or centred. Text wider than the field or with a character the font lacks turns red and blocks the download. No editing on the frame itself: hidden, and it would skip the points' suggestions. `<Brand>_plate-frame_501x21.pdf` is the field at 1:1, no bleed, the text in outlines filled with a spot colour named White (white ink for UV printing on the black frame), over a black stand-in for the frame on a layer of its own, «Фон — не печатать», set not to print (an optional content group with PrintState OFF). The plate and frame are the Figma vectors in `PlateArt.tsx` (1 px = 1 mm, frame at 195,86 of the 912×304 picture); the guide draws the strip with live `<text>`, the generator with the outlines that go into the PDF.

The PDF's title is its file name. A link with `pdf` saves `<Brand>_plate-frame_501x21.pdf` to the downloads and opens it with no editor; a line at fault opens the editor (see «Links with settings» in `docs/generators.md`).

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in the caption before its reset, `<Brand>_plate-frame_template.pdf`: the same page, the text live in the brand font Medium in the same White spot colour over the same unprinted black (`stripTemplate`); `<site>/plate-frame?t` gives «<Бренд> Центр» on the left (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
