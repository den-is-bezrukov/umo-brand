# Name tag (`/name-tag`)

A dealership employee's badge (Figma: UMO | Evrone, node 4021:2878), reproducing the hand-made source in Yandex Disk `02 UMO/Name Tag` (`UMO_name-tag.pdf`, its numbers measured off it in `tag.ts`): 70×25 mm, 4 mm corners and margins, the name and surname in CoFo Sans Medium 14 pt on 12 pt lines (baselines 7.65 and 11.88 mm), the position in Regular 9 pt on 10.8 pt lines, anchored at the bottom (its last line on 21.23 mm, a second line at 17.42), the source's UMO logo 20×4 mm in the top right corner (`LOGO`, its outline copied from the PDF; the spec forbids redrawing it).

The type size never shrinks: every line of the name and surname stays in the 40 mm column left of the logo, 2 mm clear of it (Преображенский, 39.6 mm, fits; Александровская, 40.3, is an error). The name and surname take typed line breaks, and without one a double name too long breaks after a hyphen (`splitAtHyphen`); together they take three lines at most (`NAME_LINES`), and three leave the position one; the position wraps at 62 mm or at a typed break, up to two lines. The position is a text field with a combobox (`PositionPicker` feeds `ComboField` the positions in `POSITIONS`, `src/data/positions.ts`, alphabetical, a draft for the brand team to correct): narrowed to those containing what's typed, the whole list when nothing or a listed position is typed; a manual popover placed by CSS anchor positioning (elsewhere from the field's box when it opens); arrows move, Enter picks, Esc or leaving closes, the chevron opens the whole list; combobox/listbox ARIA roles. A pick carries its line break (Продавец-консультант / новых автомобилей; `withListBreak` in `buildTag` gives a typed or pasted listed position its break too).

The page opens with one empty tag; the placeholders (Имя, Фамилия, Должность) stand on it in grey and never go into the PDF. A tag needs a name, a surname and a position. Only the field at fault turns red on the tag (`faultyFields`, from the label its issue starts with; position lines over the limit run down off the plate); a missing field is said under the tag («Нет имени», «Нет фамилии», «Нет должности»). A double click on a tag's name, surname or position edits it on the tag (`InlineField`, a transparent textarea over the field's lines in the same font, size and leading, placed from `fields` in `buildTag`'s result in container units, while the tag leaves that field's outlines out; the sidebar follows; Esc or a click elsewhere ends it; in the table mode it switches to «Вручную» first).

The template `public/downloads/UMO_name-tags_template.xlsx` (Имя, Фамилия, Должность, one example row; hand-written XML, zipped by a Python script). The zip holds `UMO_name-tags.pdf` (a 70×25 page per person: the outline as a 0.5 pt stroke for the cut, logo and text as filled outlines, C60 M40 Y40 K100), `UMO_name-tag_reference.webp` (the guide's photo of the tag on a shirt, `src/assets/guide/name-tag.webp`) and `00_UMO_name-tags_spec.txt`, the source's requirements for the maker without the steps the file does itself. The preview is a flat white plate on the grey canvas (never a silver render), the tags in one column, at most 480 px wide, centred both ways (`m-auto`: a list taller than the screen starts at the top and scrolls). The guide's photo `name-tag.webp`: Figma 4980:9768 is a 700×420 frame over a 1400×1400 original, so the original is cut to that frame at full size (1400×840 from y 280) rather than exported.
