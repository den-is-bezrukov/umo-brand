# Name tag (`/name-tag`)

A dealership employee's badge, reproducing the hand-made source in Yandex Disk `02 UMO/Name Tag` (`UMO_name-tag.pdf`, its numbers measured off it in `tag.ts`): 70×25 mm, 4 mm corners and margins, the name and surname in CoFo Sans Medium 14 pt on 12 pt lines (baselines 7.65 and 11.88 mm), the position in Regular 9 pt on 10.8 pt lines, anchored at the bottom (its last line on 21.23 mm, a second line at 17.42), the source's UMO logo 20×4 mm in the top right corner (`LOGO`, its outline copied from the PDF; the spec forbids redrawing it).

The type size never shrinks: every line of the name and surname stays in the 40 mm column left of the logo, 2 mm clear of it (Преображенский, 39.6 mm, fits; Александровская, 40.3, is an error). The name and surname take typed line breaks, and without one a double name too long breaks after a hyphen (`splitAtHyphen`); together they take three lines at most (`NAME_LINES`), and three leave the position one; the position wraps at 62 mm or at a typed break, up to two lines. The position is a text field with a combobox (`PositionPicker` feeds `ComboField` the positions in `POSITIONS` on one line (`POSITION_OPTIONS`), `src/data/positions.ts`, alphabetical, a draft for the brand team to correct): narrowed to those containing what's typed, the whole list when nothing or a listed position is typed; a manual popover placed by CSS anchor positioning (elsewhere from the field's box when it opens); arrows move, Enter picks, Esc or leaving closes, the chevron opens the whole list; combobox/listbox ARIA roles. A pick goes into the field on one line and the tag breaks it where the list does (Продавец-консультант / новых автомобилей; `withListBreak` in `buildTag`, for a typed or pasted listed position too); a typed break wins (the field showed the list's break, two lines for a position that fits one).

The page opens with one empty tag; the placeholders (Имя, Фамилия, Должность) stand on it in grey and never go into the PDF. A tag needs a name, a surname and a position. Only the field at fault turns red on the tag (`faultyFields`, from the label its issue starts with; position lines over the limit run down off the plate); a missing field is said under the tag («Нет имени», «Нет фамилии», «Нет должности»). No editing on the tag itself: a double click edited a field right there until 2026-10-13, dropped as hidden and skipping the positions' suggestions.

The template `brand/umo/public/downloads/UMO_name-tags_template.xlsx` (Имя, Фамилия, Должность, one example row; hand-written XML, zipped by a Python script). The zip holds `UMO_name-tags.pdf` (a 70×25 page per person: the outline as a 0.5 pt stroke for the cut, logo and text as filled outlines, C60 M40 Y40 K100), `UMO_name-tag_reference.webp` (the guide's photo of the tag on a shirt, `src/assets/guide/name-tag.webp`) and `00_UMO_name-tags_spec.txt`, the source's requirements for the maker without the steps the file does itself. The preview is a flat white plate on the grey canvas (never a silver render), the tags in one column, at most 480 px wide, centred both ways (`m-auto`: a list taller than the screen starts at the top and scrolls). The guide's photo `name-tag.webp`: Figma's is a 700×420 frame over a 1400×1400 original, so the original is cut to that frame at full size (1400×840 from y 280) rather than exported. Not kept in the address: a staff list isn't sent as a link; «Поделиться» sends the files themselves (see `docs/generators.md`). One person's tag can come in by a link, read on load only (`fromLink`): `name`, `surname`, `position`; `pdf` then downloads the ZIP once the fonts are in, the editor staying with the tag (a ZIP doesn't open in the browser), a tag at fault only showing what's wrong.

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in the tag's column, `UMO_name-tags_template.pdf`, a PDF of its own, not in the ZIP: that tag's page, the text live in CoFo Sans, the outline and logo as in the ZIP's PDF (`tagsTemplate`; each line's pens from `forEachGlyph`, as `getPath` sets it); `umo.autos/name-tag?t` gives one tag of placeholders (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
