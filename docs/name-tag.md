# Name tag (`/name-tag`)

A point employee's badge, reproducing the hand-made source, laid out by the brand (`BrandNameTag` in `tag.ts`, its numbers in the brand's `name-tag.ts` and `README.md`, measured off the source): the plate with rounded corners and margins, the name and surname in the brand font Medium, the position in Regular, anchored at the bottom (a second line above the first), the source's logo in the top right corner (`LOGO`, its outline copied from the PDF; the spec forbids redrawing it).

The type size never shrinks: every line of the name and surname stays in the column left of the logo, `name.clear` mm clear of it. The name and surname take typed line breaks, and without one a double name too long breaks after a hyphen (`splitAtHyphen`); together they take three lines at most (`NAME_LINES`), and three leave the position one; the position wraps at 62 mm or at a typed break, up to two lines. The position is a text field with a combobox (`PositionPicker` feeds `ComboField` the positions in `POSITIONS` on one line (`POSITION_OPTIONS`), `src/data/positions.ts`, alphabetical, a draft for the brand team to correct): narrowed to those containing what's typed, the whole list when nothing or a listed position is typed; a manual popover placed by CSS anchor positioning (elsewhere from the field's box when it opens); arrows move, Enter picks, Esc or leaving closes, the chevron opens the whole list; combobox/listbox ARIA roles. A pick goes into the field on one line and the tag breaks it where the list does (Продавец-консультант / новых автомобилей; `withListBreak` in `buildTag`, for a typed or pasted listed position too); a typed break wins (the field showed the list's break, two lines for a position that fits one).

The page opens with one empty tag; the placeholders (Имя, Фамилия, Должность) stand on it in grey and never go into the PDF. A tag needs a name, a surname and a position. Only the field at fault turns red on the tag (`faultyFields`, from the label its issue starts with; position lines over the limit run down off the plate); a missing field is said under the tag («Нет имени», «Нет фамилии», «Нет должности»). No editing on the tag itself: hidden, and it would skip the positions' suggestions.

The template `brand/<name>/public/downloads/<Brand>_name-tags_template.xlsx` (Имя, Фамилия, Должность, one example row; hand-written XML, zipped by a Python script). The zip holds `<Brand>_name-tags.pdf` (a 70×25 page per person: the outline as a 0.5 pt stroke for the cut, logo and text as filled outlines, the brand's print black (`ink` in its `colors.ts`)), `<Brand>_name-tag_reference.webp` (the guide's photo of the tag on a shirt, `brand/<name>/assets/guide/name-tag.webp`) and `00_<Brand>_name-tags_spec.txt`, the source's requirements for the maker without the steps the file does itself. The preview is a flat white plate on the grey canvas (never a silver render), the tags in one column, at most 480 px wide, centred both ways (`m-auto`: a list taller than the screen starts at the top and scrolls). The guide's photo `name-tag.webp`: Figma's is a 700×420 frame over a 1400×1400 original, so the original is cut to that frame at full size (1400×840 from y 280) rather than exported. Not kept in the address: a staff list isn't sent as a link; «Поделиться» sends the files themselves (see `docs/generators.md`). One person's tag can come in by a link, read on load only (`fromLink`): `name`, `surname`, `position`; `pdf` then downloads the ZIP once the fonts are in, the editor staying with the tag (a ZIP doesn't open in the browser), a tag at fault only showing what's wrong.

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in the tag's column, `<Brand>_name-tags_template.pdf`, a PDF of its own, not in the ZIP: that tag's page, the text live in the brand font, the outline and logo as in the ZIP's PDF (`tagsTemplate`; each line's pens from `forEachGlyph`, as `getPath` sets it); `<site>/name-tag?t` gives one tag of placeholders (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
