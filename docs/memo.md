# Owner's memo (`/memo`)

The memo a dealership hands over with a car (Figma: UMO | Evrone, section 5222:5427): two A4 pages, 2100×2970 Figma px, 10 a millimetre. Everything is Figma's but the contacts at the foot of the second page (5222:6042). `scripts/fetch-memo.mjs` (run by hand with a Figma token, `FIGMA_TOKEN=figd_… node scripts/fetch-memo.mjs`, when the memo changes in Figma) takes out both pages (5228:2, 5228:40) as SVG with the text in outlines, without the contacts, into `src/assets/memo/page-1.svg` and `page-2.svg`, every text path marked `data-text="1"`, and the texts with their styles, lists and spacing into `src/memo/texts.json`. Figma's own PDF can't serve as the template: its text is Type 3 «FigmaGlyphs», not CoFo Sans.

The contacts (`buildBlock` in `src/memo/memo.ts`): two columns from 15 and 105 mm, each 85 mm at most. The dealership stands on 282 mm (where Figma's five lines end) and grows up, the consultant hanging from its top, so the dealer's name and the position are always on one line: its name, CoFo Sans Medium 4,5 mm on 5,985 mm lines, then phone, address (up to three lines), email and site in Regular 3,6 mm on 4,5 (the fields «Телефон дилера», «Почта дилера», apart from the consultant's). The phone comes before the address, level with the consultant's name, so the address's closer lines come after the rows the columns share. The consultant: position (up to two lines) in Medium, then name and surname on one line (the name first, as on the business card; two fields, a whole name typed or pasted into «Имя» split into both, the patronymic dropped, `fullNameField`; one typed after the name by hand stays), phone and email. 1,5 mm between them; a line left out closes up. The dealer picked puts in its umo.auto address and showroom phone while they're empty or the previous dealer's, the site follows the name (`siteFor`) until edited; the dealer's fields are always open (they were under «Редактировать»): its site is to be checked by the dealer. The phones are the business card's mask. Required: dealer's name, address, phone and site, the consultant's position («Мастер-консультант» to start with, the name tag's positions offered), name, surname and phone; both emails optional. Empty required fields are grey placeholders on the page.

The dealership is the whole list's, the consultants a list (`useStaff`, as the business card's, manual only). The preview: each memo as it prints, a spread of the first page beside the consultant's second (Figma's SVG as an image, the contacts over it), 16 px apart, each page edged; the first page, everyone's, is never dimmed, only the second of a consultant not selected. On phones the first page is a slide of its own, then each consultant's second.

«Скачать PDF», `UMO_owner-memo.pdf`: two pages a consultant, everything in outlines, 100% K, the stores' frames K50; Figma's pages are one compressed form XObject each, written to 0,01 pt, placed on every page (any number of consultants ≈ 600 KB). «Скачать шаблон», beside «Добавить» under the pages, `UMO_owner-memo_template.pdf`: the same pages with every text live, CoFo Sans Regular and Medium embedded as subsets under their own names (`UMOAAA+CoFoSans-Regular`, `UMOAAB+CoFoSans-Medium`; pdf-lib's random suffix would keep Illustrator from the installed font). The font allows embedding for viewing and printing only (fsType 4), so the text is edited in Illustrator or Acrobat where CoFo Sans is installed. Each line is one text object, set by `layoutText` (`src/memo/text.ts`) as Figma sets it: the font's height (hhea) centred in the line, lines broken between words, paragraph and list spacing, an ordered list's number hung 13 px before its 55 px indent and numbered on across a section's texts until the next heading (`start`), kerned through TJ. The template takes what's typed, placeholders where nothing is, and is never blocked.
