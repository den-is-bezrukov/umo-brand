# Owner's memo (`/memo`)

The memo a dealership hands over with a car (Figma: UMO | Evrone, section 5222:5427): two A4 pages, 2100×2970 Figma px, 10 a millimetre. Everything is Figma's but the contacts at the foot of the second page (5222:6042). `scripts/fetch-memo.mjs` (run by hand with a Figma token, `FIGMA_TOKEN=figd_… node scripts/fetch-memo.mjs`, when the memo changes in Figma) takes out both pages (5228:2, 5228:40) as SVG with the text in outlines, without the contacts, into `src/assets/memo/page-1.svg` and `page-2.svg`, every text path marked `data-text="1"` (read back by `parseArt` in `src/memo/memo.ts`: Figma's absolute M L H V C Z, and Q, a quadratic curve turned into a cubic, which the hand-swapped 5 of «15 000 км» on page 1 is written in), and the texts with their styles, lists and spacing into `src/memo/texts.json`. Figma's own PDF can't serve as the template: its text is Type 3 «FigmaGlyphs», not CoFo Sans.

The contacts (`buildBlock` in `src/memo/memo.ts`): two columns on twelve of 15 mm across the 180 mm text, six and six (from 15 and 105 mm, each 85 mm at most), or, for an address that takes a third line in six, eight and four (the dealer 115 mm from 15, the consultant 60 mm from 135), so the address mostly fits in two. One layout for the whole list (`buildBlocks`): eight and four only where every consultant fits in four, the position in two lines and the rest in one, else six and six with the address in three. A line wider than its column (an email, with no spaces to break at) is a fault, «Почта шире 85 мм». The dealership stands on 282 mm (where Figma's five lines end) and grows up, the consultant hanging from its top, so the dealer's name and the position are always on one line: its name, CoFo Sans Medium 4,5 mm on 5,985 mm lines, then phone, address (up to three lines), email and site in Regular 3,6 mm on 4,5 (the fields «Телефон дилера», «Почта дилера», apart from the consultant's). The phone comes before the address, level with the consultant's name, so the address's closer lines come after the rows the columns share. The consultant: position (up to two lines) in Medium, then name and surname on one line (the name first, as on the business card; two fields, a whole name typed or pasted into «Имя» split into both, the patronymic dropped, `fullNameField`; one typed after the name by hand stays), phone and email. 1,5 mm between them; a line left out closes up. The dealer picked puts in its umo.auto address and showroom phone while they're empty or the previous dealer's, the site follows the name (`siteFor`) until edited; the dealer's fields are always open (they were under «Редактировать»): its site is to be checked by the dealer. The phones are the business card's mask. Required: dealer's name, address, phone and site, the consultant's position («Мастер-консультант» to start with, the name tag's positions offered), name, surname and phone; both emails optional. Empty required fields are grey placeholders on the page.

The dealership is the whole list's, the consultants a list (`useStaff`, as the business card's, manual only). The preview (wide screens): each memo's two pages in a row, 16 px apart, each edged, as large as the canvas lets both stand whole (600 px wide at most, `PAGE_MAX`, measured), never cut off or scrolled across. The item's actions stay on its right. Tried and dropped: the spread not fitted to the canvas, the business card's column (one page in view), one page with the pages as thumbnails on its left, the row scrolling across with one page in view. The first page, everyone's, is never dimmed, only the second of a consultant not selected. While a field in the form has the caret (wide screens), the canvas scrolls its memo's contacts (the consultant selected, or the first for the dealership's fields) to its middle when they're out of view; nothing on the page is marked or zoomed (a frame round the field, the field in blue and the second page zoomed to the item's width were tried and dropped). On phones the first page is a slide of its own, then each consultant's second.

«Скачать PDF», `UMO_owner-memo.pdf`: two pages a consultant, everything in outlines, 100% K, the stores' frames K50; Figma's pages are one compressed form XObject each, written to 0,01 pt, placed on every page (any number of consultants ≈ 600 KB). «Скачать шаблон», an icon in the consultant's column (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`; tried and dropped: a text button beside «Добавить» under the pages), `UMO_owner-memo_template.pdf`: that consultant's two pages with every text live, CoFo Sans Regular and Medium embedded whole under their own names (`CoFoSans-Regular`, `CoFoSans-Medium`; pdf-lib's random suffix would keep Illustrator from the installed font), about 250 KB. Illustrator 2026 opens a PDF of several pages by importing them all into a new document, artboard a page: with the fonts as subsets it outlined every text there (a page opened alone, «Previewed Page», kept it live), whole it keeps them live. That import names the document «Untitled-N» and leaves the view off the artboards (any PDF of several pages, a macOS one too; View → Fit All in Window, ⌥⌘0), which the file can't change. The font allows embedding for viewing and printing only (fsType 4), so the text is edited in Illustrator or Acrobat where CoFo Sans is installed. Each line is one text object, set by `layoutText` (`src/memo/text.ts`) as Figma sets it: the font's height (hhea) centred in the line, lines broken between words, paragraph and list spacing, an ordered list's number hung 13 px before its 55 px indent and numbered on across a section's texts until the next heading (`start`), kerned through TJ. The template takes what's typed, placeholders where nothing is, and is never blocked.

A link, `umo.autos/memo?t`, saves the blank template to the downloads and opens it with no editor (`useTemplateLink`, as every generator's `template`, see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`): every contact its placeholder («Название дилера», «Имя Фамилия», «Почта» for both emails), made from the memo as it is now, so the link never goes stale; Metrika `download_owner_memo_template` with `via: link`. The memo keeps no other settings in its address.
