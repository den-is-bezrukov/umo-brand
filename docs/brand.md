# The brand

Everything a brand's own lives in its folder, `brand/<name>/` or anywhere else; the rest of the code is the core, which takes the brand only from there, through the `@brand/…` alias, and names no brand. `vite.config.ts` points the alias at the `BRAND` variable's folder, else package.json's `brand`: a name is a folder in `brand/`, a path a brand's folder anywhere, absolute or from the core's folder (`BRAND=../site/brand pnpm build`, the same for `pnpm check`), so another brand is another folder with the same parts. A folder outside the core needs nothing of its own to build: its React is the core's (`resolve.dedupe`), the dev server may serve it (`server.fs.allow`). `src/brand.ts` says what each part has to hold; the brand's modules are typed by it. TypeScript checks every brand's folder against the default brand's types (`tsconfig.json`'s `@brand`, the one place besides package.json that names it). What is the brand's alone, and how its parts were made, is in the brand's own `README.md`. Figma frames for print are 1:10 by default, 10 px a millimetre (an A4 frame 2100×2970); where a file differs (the A3 price card on the same 2100×2970 frame is 7.07 px a millimetre, UMO's hours-sign frame 5), its scale is among the README's numbers.

A brand needn't have every part: one the brand has no file for is the core's empty default in `src/brand-default/` (no points, no models, no livery, a blank sign; the `brand-defaults` plugin in `vite.config.ts`), and `materials` in its `site.json` lists the generators it has, the rest left out of the routes, the generators' line, the guide's Материалы and the built pages (`MATERIALS`, `src/data/materials.ts`). `BRAND=<name> pnpm dev` runs another brand (an entry of its own in `.claude/launch.json`, on a port of its own).

The core is written as if every brand were a client: no brand's name, font, site, points or Figma file in the core's code, comments, docs or skills; examples say «<Бренд>», «Точка Город», `<Brand>_price-tags.pdf`, «the brand font».

## Parts

- `public/` — the site's root: the fonts, the guide's downloads (`downloads/`), the icons, the social image. The core's own `public/` holds only `404.html`, added to the build by `core-public`.
- `theme.css` — the site's type and greys: the `@font-face` rules, Tailwind's `--font-sans` (the guide's type, the brand's own) and `--font-editor` (the editor's, on the generators' pages: a free font, the brand type's alternative from Google Fonts, whole, as its release ships it, or the guide's own type where the site serves it anyway; `:root[data-editor]` in `src/index.css`, set by `src/main.tsx` while a generator is open), and the interface's tokens the whole site is drawn with, by role, every one the brand's: the colours `ink` (the text, and at its opacities the edges, rings, underlines and dims), `paper` (the page, panels and menus), `surface` (fields and backgrounds), `line` (hairlines), `caption` (captions and quiet text), `hint` (placeholders and what can't be pressed yet), `faint` (the generators' names not open), `ghost` (the empty fields in a preview), `accent` and `on-accent` (the main button and its text), `error` (a field at fault), `success` (a link that's right), as classes `text-ink`, `bg-paper`, `border-ink/10`, `fill-ghost`; the corners `radius-control` (`rounded-control`: fields, buttons, switches, menus), `radius-panel` (windows and cards) and `radius-sheet` (the generators' sheet on a phone). What the core keeps: sizes, spacing and the grid; the drop shadows, black; the materials' own colours in their previews (their paper and print black, the red marking text at fault on a car or a plate, the livery's orange dimensions). `src/index.css` imports it. A brand may restyle the editor past the tokens with CSS of its own imported from its `theme.css` (an `editor.css`, say): scoped to `:root[data-editor]`, where it may also set the tokens for the editor alone, and written against the parts the core names with `data-ui`: `sidebar`, `sidebar-body`, `form`, `field` and its `label`, `input`, `segments` and `seg`, `chevron` and a suggestions' `list` (`option`, `data-active` while pointed at or chosen by the arrows, its `picked` mark), `valid`, the tick of a QR link that's right, `checkbox`, `button`, `primary` and `download`, `crumbs`, `ring` and `ring-name`, and a head of its own the core keeps hidden, `head-bar` (`head-logo` to the guide, `head-name`, `head-menu` for every generator) and `head-title`, the open generator's name (and `data-head`); a field may carry `data-part` (`url` the QR link, `rear-tagline` the livery's rear window slogan). Those names are the core's promise to keep; its classes are not, so a brand's CSS never names them.
- `colors.ts` — `COLORS`, the guide's Цвет: the main colours in large tiles, the service ones in small, each with its values (screen, print); and `ink`, the print black of the film, signs and a black ground (the livery, the name tag, the hours sign, the business card's black face), text on white staying 100% K.
- `fonts.ts` — `FONTS`, the type the generators set their files in (in outlines, `src/ui/type.ts`) and their editable templates in (live, `src/ui/liveTextPdf.ts`, the memo's `src/memo/pdf.ts`): the TrueType file and PostScript name of the Regular and the Medium, `family`, the type's name as people know it (the instructions say to install it for the templates), and `short`, which names them in a PDF's resources. A type of one weight gives the same face as both: the generators set everything in it, a template embeds it once, and `theme.css` declares it for every weight (`font-weight: 100 900`), so the guide never fakes a bold.

- `logo.ts` — `LOGO`, the wordmark in outlines for the generators (the business card, price tag, hours sign), cut to its ink.
- `name-tag.ts` — `NAME_TAG_LOGO`, the name tag's source drawing of the logo in the tag's millimetres.
- `business-card.ts` — `BUSINESS_CARD` (`BrandBusinessCard` in `src/card/card.ts`): the card's size, faces to choose from (white or black, the logo on it), the back's logo, the QR, the point block if the card names one, and the person's lines (`docs/business-card.md`).
- `hours-sign.ts` — `HOURS_SIGN` (`BrandHoursSign` in `src/hours/sign.ts`): the sheet, the form's fields and the variants, each a list of blocks — the logo, the hours, lines and columns of rows, their texts templates of the fields («Адрес: {address}») (`docs/hours-sign.md`).
- `guide/` — the guide's content: `index.tsx` holds the chapters in the contents (`CHAPTERS`, before the core's Материалы and Контакты), old anchors, the first screen (`HERO`, `STATEMENT`), Материалы' lead, order and pictures (`MATERIALS_LEAD`, `INTRO_ORDER`, `DRAWN`), the chapters themselves (`Chapters`, written with `src/guide/parts.tsx`), Контакты' text and the footer; `images/` the pictures and the film, `images.ts` their list for `img()`.
- `assets/` — the generators' pictures: the cars for the price card, the livery's sides, the memo's pages (with the memo's texts, `memo/texts.json`).
- `help.tsx` — «Инструкция»'s texts (`GENERAL`, `HELP` per generator, `MAKES` after «Как сделать»), written with `src/ui/helpParts.tsx` (`Bot`, `CopyLink`, `TEMPLATE`); the core's default speaks of points, products and vehicles in `words.ts`' words and nothing of a brand's own layouts, prices or fixed lines.
- `positions.ts` — `POSITIONS`, the positions the name tag and business card offer, a line break where the layouts break one (the core's default: a few common to any office).
- `words.ts` — `WORDS`, what the brand calls its points in the editor's phrases (`point`: «Дилер», «Дилеры», «дилера» at UMO; the core's default «Точка»; see «Points» in `docs/generators.md`).
- `points.ts` — `POINTS`, the points (UMO's dealers): lists merged in order (where they come from is the brand's), the prefix every name starts with (the brand's name), the brand's name as people write it; `src/data/points.ts` merges and searches them.
- `site.json` — the site's settings, `SITE` (`src/site.ts`): `materials` (the generators the brand has, by path; none for every one), `names` (a generator as the brand names it, over the core's: UMO's «памятка владельца», «ливрея демо-автомобиля»), `name` («Бренд <Бренд>», «Ценник <Бренд>»), `file` (the files' prefix, <Brand>_price-tags.pdf), `domain` (where the site lives: the instructions' links, Metrika's host), `home` (the brand's own site: a QR's default, the points' sites under it), `support` (the bot), `metrika` (the counter, none for none), and the HTML's title, description, language, robots, icons and social image, which `vite.config.ts` reads.
- `products.ts` — `PRODUCTS`, the products the price card advertises (UMO's models): id (in links and data), name («<Бренд> 5», also on files: <Brand>-5-PRO.pdf), page (a QR's default); the first is the default; `src/data/products.ts` reads them. What the brand calls a product and its variant is its `words.ts` (`product`: «Модель», «Комплектация» at UMO; the core's «Продукт», «Вариант»).
- `price-card.ts` — `PRICE_CARD`, what the price card says per model: the headline (a run may have its own font and colour), the wordmark with the model's number, the trims with their photos, prices, lists and fine print; `src/pricecard/card.ts` lays it out.
- `taglines.ts` — the livery's slogans per model, the default first.
- `livery.ts`, `lettering.ts` — `LIVERY`, the livery's vehicles (`vehicles`, as products are written: UMO's are its products; files <Brand>5_livery.zip, UMO's UMO5_dealer-livery.zip; their field's label `words.ts`' `vehicle`): the wordmark (600×120) and per model the surfaces in mm (sides and rear window, QR, lettering, text zones, obstacles, photos, spec dimensions), the model numbers' outlines in `lettering.ts`; `src/livery/surface.ts` holds the mechanics and the types they're written in.
- `Wordmark.tsx`, `HeaderLogo.tsx`, `CobrandLockup.tsx` — the guide's logo: the wordmark on plates, the header's lockup that folds with the scroll (`COBRAND` turns the partner off; `setLogoFold` folds it by script, its layers' moves in the brand's `theme.css`), the co-branding lockup.
- `check.mjs` — the check's scenarios on the brand's data (`SCENARIOS`, `TABLES`, read by `scripts/check.mjs`).
- `README.md` — the brand's own notes: its numbers and how its parts were made.

A demo brand may implement its editor as a theme over the same working generators; its temporary print layouts and guide readiness are recorded in its own README. The default build is still selected independently by package.json.
