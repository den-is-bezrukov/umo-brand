# Price card (`/price-card`)

`src/App.tsx`. Default full prices per model and trim are in `DEFAULTS`; the credit price follows the full one, a million less (`creditFor`), until set by hand, and is off by default (the «В кредит» checkbox): the card then shows one price under «Цена:», 96 px, its figures standing on the QR code's bottom line — the dark modules' edge, a quiet-zone module above the code's box (`singlePriceTop` in `PriceCard` and `PriceCardPdf`; `creditPrice` is optional in both). The QR leads to the model's own page by default (`DEFAULT_URL`: https://umo.auto/umo8, https://umo.auto/umo5), and switching models swaps it while it's still the old model's page.

The page holds several cards (`useStaff` with no table mode, opened with the link's cards through `initial`): one always selected, each fitting the canvas's height on wide screens (a plain box clipped to the card); a card's «Сбросить» brings it back to its model's defaults; «Добавить» adds a copy of the last card (`add(from)`). «Скачать» gives one PDF, an A3 page per card (`PriceCardPdf` takes `pages`; one card is `UMO-8-MAX.pdf`, several `UMO_price-cards.pdf`). Faults (`firstIssue`): «Цена слишком низкая» (under 999 999, `MIN_PRICE`), «Полная цена меньше цены в кредит», «Проверьте ссылку QR-кода». On phones model and trim share a row while each is at least 180 px wide, else stand one under the other (`auto-fit` columns); the prices always stand one under the other, the «В кредит» checkbox under the full price. Emptied, the fields show 9 999 999 and 8 999 999 grey.

The car photos live at fixed paths so they can be swapped without touching code: `src/assets/umo5-car.jpg` and `src/assets/umo8-car.jpg`. The PDF is printed at A3 and the photo must hold up at 300dpi — don't shrink it to "web size": **3840×2160 for UMO5** and **3520×1980 for UMO8**, JPEG quality ~90 (about 1.5–2 MB). Replace the file in place, same filename.

Chat agents make it as a link with `pdf=1` (see «Chat agents» in `docs/generators.md`); `public/llms.txt` repeats the trims, the `DEFAULTS` prices and the link's parameters, so change it with them.

Trial: an offer stands in the download's group, between «Поделиться» and the download, 8 px apart as they are: «Сделать с @ Алисой AI» on an untouched form (one card, UMO 5 defaults), «Продолжить с @ Алисой AI» once anything changes, a secondary button as «Поделиться» (black text, a 10% black border), only her mark purple (#7A55FF), with a grey ×, which hides it until the page is reloaded; 48 px tall in the phone's sheet, as the buttons there. It opens Alice with public/llms.txt in her field, as umo.autos/ai does, after a line saying it's the price card (untouched: «начни сразу с вопросов о ней»; else each card's model, trim, prices and QR link, and to ask only what's left), and sends `ai_open` with `chat: 'alice'` and `from` (`AliceOffer` in `src/ui/form.tsx`). The other generators have the offer too (`DownloadBar`'s default), always «Сделать», with the line saying which editor the dealer is in. Also a trial, in every generator: the menu (`GeneratorHeader`) has the same button, full width, with no ×, at the sidebar's foot on wide screens (24 px up, as the download; at least 40 px under the generators), with the same line here and, in the other generators, which editor the dealer is in (they're made there, so she helps with it); its goal has `via: 'menu'`.
