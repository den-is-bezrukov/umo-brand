# Price card (`/price-card`)

`src/App.tsx`. Default full prices per model and trim are in `DEFAULTS`; the credit price follows the full one, a million less (`creditFor`), until set by hand, and is off by default (the «В кредит» checkbox): the card then shows one price under «Цена:», 96 px, its figures standing on the QR code's bottom line — the dark modules' edge, a quiet-zone module above the code's box (`singlePriceTop` in `PriceCard` and `PriceCardPdf`; `creditPrice` is optional in both). The QR leads to the model's own page by default (`DEFAULT_URL`: https://umo.auto/umo8, https://umo.auto/umo5), and switching models swaps it while it's still the old model's page.

The page holds several cards (`useStaff` with no table mode, opened with the link's cards through `initial`): one always selected, each fitting the canvas's height on wide screens (a plain box clipped to the card); a card's «Сбросить» brings it back to its model's defaults; «Добавить», the plus in the card's column (⌘↩), adds a copy of the last card (`add(from)`). «Скачать» gives one PDF, an A3 page per card (`PriceCardPdf` takes `pages`; one card is `UMO-8-MAX.pdf`, several `UMO_price-cards.pdf`). The PDF code (react-pdf, `pdf/cardsPdf.tsx`, 450 KB gzipped) loads on the first download or share, as the other generators' PDF code does, not with the page; the button's «Генерация…» covers the wait. The car photos are two files each: the full JPEG (`umo5-car.jpg` 3840 px, `umo8-car.jpg` 2752 px) goes into the PDF (`image` in `cardData.ts`), a 1600 px WebP copy (`*-car-preview.webp`, ~120 KB) into the preview (`preview`); a new photo needs both, the copy made with `cwebp -q 80 -resize 1600 0`. Faults (`firstIssue`): «Цена слишком низкая» (under 999 999, `MIN_PRICE`), «Полная цена меньше цены в кредит», «Проверьте ссылку QR-кода». On phones model and trim share a row while each is at least 180 px wide, else stand one under the other (`auto-fit` columns); the prices always stand one under the other, the «В кредит» checkbox under the full price. Emptied, the fields show 9 999 999 and 8 999 999 grey.

The car photos live at fixed paths so they can be swapped without touching code: `src/assets/umo5-car.jpg` and `src/assets/umo8-car.jpg`. The PDF is printed at A3 and the photo must hold up at 300dpi — don't shrink it to "web size": **3840×2160 for UMO5** and **3520×1980 for UMO8**, JPEG quality ~90 (about 1.5–2 MB). Replace the file in place, same filename.

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in the card's column, `UMO_price-card_template.pdf`: that card, one A3 page, its layout as set (model, one price or two), its texts as placeholders in brackets («[Дескриптор модели] с Алисой ☺ и сервисами Яндекса. Запас хода до [000] км…», «[Название комплектации]:», «[Описание комплектации…]», «[0 000 000] ₽»), the model's digit a light grey block of its size, the photo a light checkerboard (`FISH`, `Fish` in `PriceCardPdf.tsx`, `template`); react-pdf sets its text live anyway, CoFo Sans in subsets (one page, which Illustrator keeps live), about 25 KB. `umo.autos/price-card?t` gives the default UMO 5 card's (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
