# Dealer livery (`/livery`)

Lettering for a dealer's demo car: both sides and the rear window, with the dealer name, tagline and QR link editable. It reproduces the brand's hand-made files, with the decals in one file: the zip holds the template (below), `<Brand>8_dealer-livery.pdf` (left and right side and the rear window, each sheet at its own size) and `00_<Brand>8_dealer-livery_spec.pdf` (the sheets on the car photos with dimensions in orange; one point there is one millimetre on the car).

**Parts**: the sides are always in; «QR-код», «Дилер» and «Слоган» are checkboxes that label their fields (`OptionalField`), and «Заднее стекло» adds the rear window, off by default. What's off is in neither the preview, nor the decals, nor the spec. Ticked, the rear window shows under it, not indented, «Слоган на стекле» (its field following the sides' slogan until edited) and «Дилер на стекле» (the sides' name, on or off). Turning the rear window off forgets its settings.

**Layout**: everything is in millimetres: the mechanics in `src/livery/surface.ts`, the brand's cars in `brand/<name>/livery.ts` (`LIVERY`: the wordmark and each model's surfaces; the brand's own numbers are in its `README.md`): per surface the QR, the wordmark and model number, two text blocks (dealer, tagline) and the obstacles. Text is the brand font's Medium, at each surface's size, anchored at the last line's baseline so a block grows upwards (a one-line dealer name on the sides is centred on its two lines' zone, `centred`); measured off the Illustrator sources within half a millimetre, except the bottom tagline baselines, raised by 2 mm so a descender in the last line stays on the sheet. Lines wrap by ink width; words of one or two characters stick to the next word. Pasted text is cleaned first (`clean` in `geometry.ts`): every kind of space becomes a plain one and zero-width marks go (the narrow no-break space has no glyph in the brand font), and a character the font lacks is an error. Limits are each surface's: how wide and how many lines the dealer and the tagline may take (on a side clear of the door handle and seam); on the rear one dealer line (typed line breaks become spaces) and one tagline line more without a dealer name. Anything over the limit or within `clearance` of an obstacle turns red and blocks the download.

**Sides without the QR** (`noQr`, `withoutQr`): the lettering larger on a shorter sheet from the front edge, which stays where it was; the bottom text in its usual size or, with «Крупнее» at the end of its label row (`SizeSwitch`), larger in fewer lines (`taglineLarge`). **Small QR** («Мельче» on the QR's label row): laid out as without the QR, with a small QR level with the lettering behind the model number at the rear end (`smallQr`), the sheet that much longer towards the rear, both tagline sizes offered. A small QR scans only up close (~1,5 m against ~3,5 m). The rear window keeps its QR.

**Rear window**: the text is stacked (`stack`): the tagline follows the dealer name or takes its place, and the sheet is cut down to the column (from the top of the lettering to the last descender), centred where the full sheet was. The QR is as tall as the column (`sized` in `geometry.ts`): with less text it shrinks with it, down to the lettering's height, the sheet narrowing by as much. Without the QR the column alone is cut out, centred. Its spec rows on the text side come from where the column lands (`stackDims`), to half a millimetre, the last row taking up the rounding; the full height is given on the other side.

**Side sheets** are cut down to their content (`trimmed`): without the top text a sheet starts at the top of the QR and lettering, without the bottom text and the QR it ends under the lettering; it stays where it was on the body, so the strip cut off joins the distance to the body. The spec gives the distances to the body (from the window line and to the sill moulding, along the moulding), and the rows of a text left out close up (`part` in `dims`, `withoutParts`). Without the QR its dimensions are in `noQr.dims`.

**Preview**: the sheets on the car photos, straight on the canvas (the side photos are cut out; the rear ones stay rectangles), each with one #808080 caption 10 px under it, «Левый борт, 1280 × 500 мм», the reset at its end. «Показать размеры» at the foot of the sidebar adds the spec's dimensions in orange, #ee4400, as readable on the white body as the old red and brighter on dark glass (red, #ff2a1a, is for text at fault) (`specMarks`, which the spec PDF draws too): on the page half-pixel lines on Retina screens (a whole pixel elsewhere) and figures in the brand font Medium, tabular; the spec PDF has the same figures in outlines (`labelCmds`, proportional: opentype.js doesn't apply `tnum`), its lines a fortieth of the label size.

The obstacles — the seam between the doors and the front door handle — are each model's, in `brand/<name>/livery.ts` (the right side mirrors the left). The side and rear pictures are the brand's car photos (`brand/<name>/assets/livery/`), cropped with known placement; `photo.x`/`photo.y` is where the sheet sits on them.

Cut files are vector only: every shape is a filled outline (text converted with opentype.js, the QR traced into merged contours so the plotter doesn't cut between modules), C60 M40 Y40 K100, even-odd fill.

A model the car of which is white has black side decals in the preview and spec (`decal` on a surface; the cut file's colour is the same for all); on the glass they stay white.

**Texts**: the QR leads to the model's own page by default (`DEFAULT_URL`, the model's `page` in `brand/<name>/models.ts`), swapped with the model while it's still the old one's. Each model has its own default tagline; the bottom text fields are comboboxes offering it and the brand's other slogans (`TAGLINES` in `brand/<name>/taglines.ts`, matched across the models by their place in the list); switching models swaps a suggestion for the other model's and leaves the dealer's own text alone. The dealer field starts as «Центр <Бренд>» (`DEFAULT_TOP`) and offers the dealers' marketing names, listed without «<Бренд> » and put in whole (`shownAs`).

«Скачать шаблон», an icon in every sheet's caption before its reset, each the same file (sought by the sheet in hand), `<Brand>8_dealer-livery_template.pdf` (`<Brand>5_…`), also in the ZIP, so the maker gets it with the decals and the spec: the cut file's pages, the dealer and tagline live in the brand font in C60 M40 Y40 K100, the QR and lettering in outlines, for editing the text only, as the plotter cuts outlines (`liveryTemplate`; each line keeps its text as `live`, moved with the stacked rear window); the icon and `<site>/livery?t` alike give the model's three sheets at their defaults, the rear window too, whatever's typed or switched off on the page (see «Editable templates» in `docs/generators.md`).
