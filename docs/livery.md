# Dealer livery (`/livery`)

Lettering for a dealer's demo car (Figma: UMO | Evrone, node 4021:2908): both sides and the rear window, with the dealer name, tagline and QR link editable. It reproduces the hand-made files in Yandex Disk `02 UMO/Livery/UMO 8`, with the decals in one file: the zip holds `UMO8_dealer-livery.pdf` (left and right side, 1280×500 mm each, and the rear window, 835×250 mm) and `00_UMO8_dealer-livery_spec.pdf` (the sheets on the car photos with dimensions in red; one point there is one millimetre on the car).

**Parts** (Figma 4900:4588): the sides are always in; «QR-код», «Дилер» and «Слоган» are checkboxes that label their fields (`OptionalField`), and «Заднее стекло» adds the rear window, off by default. What's off is in neither the preview, nor the decals, nor the spec. Ticked, the rear window shows under it, not indented, «Слоган на стекле» (its field following the sides' slogan until edited) and «Дилер на стекле» (the sides' name, on or off). Turning the rear window off forgets its settings.

**Layout**: everything is in millimetres in `src/livery/layout.ts`: per surface the QR, the UMO wordmark and model number, two text blocks (dealer, tagline) and the obstacles. Text is CoFo Sans Medium, 40 mm on 40 mm lines on the sides and 37.5 on the rear, anchored at the last line's baseline so a block grows upwards (a one-line dealer name on the sides is centred on its two lines' zone, `centred`, Figma 4934:3325); measured off the Illustrator sources within half a millimetre, except the bottom tagline baselines, raised by 2 mm so a descender in the last line stays on the sheet. Lines wrap by ink width; words of one or two characters stick to the next word. Pasted text is cleaned first (`clean` in `geometry.ts`): every kind of space becomes a plain one and zero-width marks go (the narrow no-break space has no glyph in CoFo Sans), and a character the font lacks is an error. Limits: on the sides the dealer is up to 500 mm and two lines (clear of the door handle), the tagline up to 400 mm and three lines (clear of the door seam); on the rear one dealer line (typed line breaks become spaces) and two tagline lines within 510 mm, three without a dealer name. Anything over the limit or within `clearance` of an obstacle turns red and blocks the download.

**Sides without the QR** (`noQr`, `withoutQr`; Figma 4920:39): the lettering a third larger, 160 mm, a 1100×500 mm sheet from the front edge, which stays where it was, so the door seam falls between UMO and 8 on the left and between U and M on the right; the bottom text 40 mm in three lines (up to 400 mm) or, with «Крупнее» at the end of its label row (`SizeSwitch`, Figma 4900:4662), 60 mm in two (up to 850 mm; `taglineLarge`). **Small QR** («Мельче» on the QR's label row; Figma 4991:10211 for UMO 8, 4985:10180 for UMO 5): laid out as without the QR, with a 160 mm QR level with the lettering, 100 mm behind the model number at the rear end (`smallQr`), the sheet 260 longer towards the rear (1360 on UMO 8, 1346,67 on UMO 5), both tagline sizes offered. A small QR scans only up close (~1,5 m against ~3,5 m). The rear window keeps its QR.

**Rear window**: the text is stacked (`stack`): the tagline follows the dealer name or takes its place, and the sheet is cut down to the column (from the top of the lettering to the last descender), centred where the full sheet was. The QR is as tall as the column (`sized` in `geometry.ts`): with all the text 250; with less it shrinks with it (UMO 8 without the dealer name 197,5, without any text the lettering's 75), the sheet narrowing by as much. Without the QR the column alone is cut out (510 wide), centred. Its spec rows on the text side come from where the column lands (`stackDims`), to half a millimetre, the last row taking up the rounding; the full height is given on the other side.

**Side sheets** are cut down to their content (`trimmed`): without the top text a sheet starts at the top of the QR and lettering, without the bottom text and the QR it ends under the lettering; it stays where it was on the body, so the strip cut off joins the distance to the body. The spec gives the distances to the body (~100 from the window line, ~150 to the sill moulding, at the front door's edge; ~125 / sheet / ~320 along the moulding), and the rows of a text left out close up (`part` in `dims`, `withoutParts`). Without the QR its dimensions are in `noQr.dims`.

**Preview**: the sheets on the car photos, straight on the canvas (the side photos are cut out; the rear ones stay rectangles), each with one #808080 caption 10 px under it, «Левый борт, 1280 × 500 мм», the reset at its end. «Показать размеры» at the foot of the sidebar adds the spec's red dimensions (`specMarks`, which the spec PDF draws too): on the page half-pixel lines on Retina screens (a whole pixel elsewhere) and figures in CoFo Sans Medium, tabular; the spec PDF has the same figures in outlines (`labelCmds`, proportional: opentype.js doesn't apply `tnum`), its lines a fortieth of the label size. «Швы» (door seam and handle) is commented out in `Livery.tsx`; `show=seams` in a link is ignored while it's out.

The obstacles — the seam between the doors and the front door handle — are approximate, taken from the spec mockup; correct them in `leftObstacles` once there are real measurements (the right side mirrors them). The side and rear pictures (`src/assets/livery/umo8-side.webp`, `umo8-rear.webp`) are the photos from the spec PDF, cropped with known placement: the side covers 4800×2000 mm of the spec page from (0, 450), the rear 2280×2400 from (300, 250); `photo.x`/`photo.y` is where the sheet sits on them.

Cut files are vector only: every shape is a filled outline (text converted with opentype.js, the QR traced into merged contours so the plotter doesn't cut between modules), C60 M40 Y40 K100, even-odd fill.

**UMO 5** (`UMO5`; Figma 3532:454 for the sides with the QR and the rear window, 4934:3338 without the QR), picked by the model switch (`LIVERIES`). The sides are 1310×500: a 830 lettering column (UMO 600, 90, the 5 140 — the 5 of the number plate, `FIVE` in `logo.ts`), 120, the 360 QR column; text 40 mm on 42 mm lines, the dealer up to 550 and two lines, the tagline up to 420 and three. Without the QR: the lettering 160 (1086,67 wide), the dealer 40 mm up to 550 in two lines or one centred (4934:3325), the tagline 40 mm in three lines up to 420 (4934:3341) or 60 mm in two up to 850. The rear window is 650×200: QR 200, 50, a 400 column with the lettering 60 high and 30 mm text, stacked like UMO 8's. The car is white, so the side decals are black in the preview and spec (`decal` on a surface; the cut file's colour is the same for both); on the glass they stay white. The pictures come from the Figma frames at 0.5 px/mm: `umo5-side.webp` is the right side (4718×1680 mm, transparent around the car, the sheet's top at 682), mirrored for the left; `umo5-rear.webp` (2080×1980 mm) has the decal painted out of the darkened window. The seam and handle in `right5Obstacles` were measured on that photo.

**Texts**: the QR leads to the model's own page by default (`DEFAULT_URL`), swapped with the model while it's still the old one's. Each model has its own default tagline («Попробуй гибрид…» / «Попробуй электрокар…»); the bottom text fields are comboboxes offering it, the same without «Попробуй» and «Гибрид / Электрокар с Яндексом внутри» (`TAGLINES` in `src/data/taglines.ts`, matched across the models by their place in the list); switching models swaps a suggestion for the other model's and leaves the dealer's own text alone. The dealer field starts as «Центр UMO» (`DEFAULT_TOP`) and offers the dealers' marketing names, listed without «UMO » and put in whole (`shownAs`); 12 of the 55 run over 500 mm on the sides and need a hand-made line break or a shorter name.
